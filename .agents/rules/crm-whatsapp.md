# WhatsApp

Read this before changing the company's WhatsApp numbers in the CRM: its webhook, the conversations, a record's WhatsApp tab and timeline lines, what the follow-ups read from WhatsApp, or its connection page.

Phase 1 receives: messages from customers to the company's numbers come in through Meta's Cloud API and are read in the CRM. Phase 2 has begun with replies within the window (below); templates, consent and usage are still to come. Coexistence, a number also used on the WhatsApp Business app, is received too (since 2026-10-16), its onboarding through Embedded Signup still to come: what the team sends from the app (`smb_message_echoes`) and the chat history imported once after onboarding (`history`) arrive as messages of the same conversations.

**Webhook.** `server/routes/whatsapp.ts` contributes a root route (`defineRootRoutes`, so `/<base path>/webhooks/whatsapp`, outside `/api`): GET answers Meta's subscription check with `hub.challenge` only for the verify token the CRM made; POST is public because Meta presents no session, and keeps a delivery only when `X-Hub-Signature-256` is the app secret's HMAC of the raw body (`verifySignature` in `server/providers/crm/whatsapp/channel.ts`), storing it as it came in `crmWaInbox` and answering 200 at once; unsigned or wrongly signed posts get 401. Meta redelivers for up to seven days and in no order: the pass processes deliveries in arrival order and messages by Meta's timestamp, and drops a message whose `waMessageId` is stored (unique index). A delivery that fails five times is `failed` with a code (`unknownNumber`, `invalidJson`, `processingFailed`; anything unexpected is logged, never stored), shown on the connection page and retried from there.

**The channel.** `channel.ts` is Meta's Cloud API alone (Graph `v25.0`): `webhookEvents` reads a delivery into messages and statuses (each message one `readMessage`, with its `direction` and `origin`: the `messages` field a customer's, `in`/`customer`; `smb_message_echoes` the team's from the app, `out`/`businessApp`, its customer the echo's `to`/`to_user_id`, an `edit` naming its message and its new words, a `revoke` naming the one deleted; `history` either way, `imported`, a message from its thread's number the customer's and any other the team's, a file a `media_placeholder` its own later delivery fills, a declined share's `errors` nothing) (business-scoped `user_id`/`from_user_id`, the number when given, username and profile name, media ids and the five-minute `url`, a `user_changed_number` system message), `cloudApiChannel` downloads media (checking `sha256`, refusing over 100 MB) and reads a number's standing. Everything else depends only on its types, so a provider proxying the Cloud API replaces the base address, the authorization and the signature check there. Tests fake `fetch`.

**The service.** `WhatsAppService` (`server/providers/crm/whatsapp/service.ts`, `whatsappToken`, registered and wired by `CrmProvider`) keeps the connection: one Meta app, its app secret and verify token in the `crmSettings` row `whatsapp` (`saveApp`; the verify token made the first time), so one webhook whose every delivery `accept` checks with that secret, stamping `lastWebhookAt` on the numbers the delivery names; and the company's numbers, as many as it has (since 2026-10-16), each a `crmWaNumbers` row (`numbers()`, mode `api` or `coexistence`) with its own access token sealed in it and its standing (`addNumber`, `updateNumber` keeping a token left out, `removeNumber`, `check(id)`; a `phoneNumberId` taken already is `numberExists` 409, a number holding conversations stays, `numberInUse` 409, to keep its customers' history). The table held a row per number from the start, so multiple numbers needed no migration. Secrets are sealed by the secrets service, purpose `crm.whatsapp`; saving needs `SECRETS_KEYS`, else `secretsMissing` 503, and runs a pass every 10 s on `CrmProvider`'s timers (`process()`: deliveries, then media, then follow-ups). A customer (`crmWaContacts`) is found by their user id (`crmWaIdentities`, every id they have had; a changed number adds the new id and marks the old replaced), else their number (`waId`), else created; a new one is linked when exactly one unconverted lead or contact has their number (`samePhone` from `lead-merge.ts`), else stays `unlinked`. States: `unlinked`, `linked`, `dismissed` (not a customer), `spam`. A conversation is a number × customer (`crmWaConversations`: last inbound time for the 24-hour window; the awaiting-reply count `unreadCount`; `followUpDueAt`); messages are `crmWaMessages` (direction, origin `customer`/`crm`, and `businessApp`/`imported` reserved for coexistence; media id, status `pending`/`stored`/`unavailable` and the CRM file it was stored as).

**Coexistence's messages.** `receive` keeps them in their conversation, so the timeline lines count both ways, but only a customer's live message (`in`, `customer`) counts toward awaiting a reply and starts the quiet follow-up; the team's from the app counts as contact on the lead (`leadWorked`), the history touches nothing but the conversation's last message and its lines. A placeholder takes its file when the same message id arrives with one; an edit sets the message's words and `editedAt`, a deletion `revokedAt`, neither stored as a message of its own and nothing done when the CRM does not hold the message. A deleted message shows as deleted (`whatsapp.revoked`), in the conversation list too (the server's last-message type `revoked`), and is left out of the follow-ups' material; the bubbles say 手机发出 or 历史导入 beside the time, and 已编辑.

**Replying from the CRM.** Within 24 hours of the customer's last message (Meta's customer service window, `lastInboundAt`, `REPLY_WINDOW_MS`) the team replies from the conversation: words, one file, or both, the words then its caption (`WhatsAppService.send`, `POST /api/whatsapp/conversations/:conversationId/messages`, multipart `text` and `file` of at most 20 MiB). It needs the page grant, read access to the conversation (`mayRead`) and the capability `sendWhatsApp` on `crm.sales` (both sales roles hold it: the role definitions in `resources.ts`, and sets from before once at boot, marker `crm-whatsapp-send-v1`; the workspace reports it as `sendWhatsApp`). After the window it is `windowClosed`, a customer known only by a username `noRecipient` (sending by business-scoped id is not done yet), both a 400 `FAILED_PRECONDITION`. The channel uploads a file to Meta first (`/{phone-number-id}/media`), a JPEG or PNG then going as a photo and anything else as a document, and posts the message (`/{phone-number-id}/messages`); Meta's id becomes the message's `waMessageId`, its statuses (sent, delivered, read, failed) arriving by webhook as for any message (`status()`). The message is kept with origin `crm`, `sentById`, its file stored with the CRM's files; it is the team's reply as one made on the app is: the conversation stops awaiting a reply, the stretch on the timeline counts it, and it counts as contact on the lead (`leadWorked`). Meta refusing it (or the network failing) keeps it as `failed` with the reason in `errorCode` (`meta_<code>`, `meta_131047` the window having closed), the conversation still awaiting a reply. On a demo number the message is kept, `sent`, and nothing leaves; the window does not apply there. The reply box (`client/components/crm/whatsapp/composer.tsx`) sits under the conversation on the WhatsApp page and in its drawer: Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a line, a paperclip attaches one file; above it the window's time left (or, on a demo number, that nothing leaves), and in its place why there is none: no permission, the window closed (a coexistence number: reply on the phone), no number. A record's WhatsApp tab says to open the conversation to reply. A message sent from the CRM shows who sent it and a status icon after its time (sending, sent, delivered, read, failed with Meta's code; `SendStatus` in `messages.tsx`).

**Deleting a conversation.** Whoever manages the CRM's settings (the connection's managers, `settingsAccess` on the client) may delete a conversation, such as one a test made (since 2026-10-20): `DELETE /api/whatsapp/conversations/:conversationId`, `WhatsAppService.removeConversation`, from Delete conversation at the foot of the conversation header's menu after a confirmation, the page then going back to the list. Its messages go, and its customer's timeline lines are written again from their conversations on other numbers (`rewriteTimeline`); a customer left with none goes too, with their ids and lines, so a later message starts a new customer and conversation. The files go with the file sweep, no message naming them; the deliveries stay in `crmWaInbox`, processed, and are never read again. It is the way to empty a test number so it can be removed (`numberInUse`).

**Awaiting a reply.** There is no unread: replies are made on the phone, so what matters is whether the team has answered. `unreadCount` is the customer's messages since the team's last live reply: a customer's live message adds one, and the team's live reply (`businessApp` now, `crm` once the CRM sends) at or after the last inbound sets it to 0. Opening a conversation (`POST …/read`, `markRead`) clears it only on a number of mode `api`, whose team's replies the CRM never sees; on a coexistence number and the demo's only a reply does, so the client posts it for `api` numbers alone. The list marks each one 待回复 (`whatsapp.awaiting`, a `warning` badge, the count of messages in its title).

**Records and timeline.** A linked customer reaches the lead, or the contact with the contact's company and the deals naming the contact (`targetsOf`). Each reached record gets one `crmHistory` row a stretch of conversation, as a mail thread gets one: a customer's messages on every number, a new stretch when someone writes after three quiet days (`STRETCH_GAP_MS`; a line a day until 2026-10-16, which flooded the timeline of a long conversation). The row has `action` and `source` `whatsapp`, `sourceKey` `<customer id>:<first message's time>`, actor `system`, its time the last message's, and `changes` with the counts in and out, who wrote last, the span (`from`, `to`) and the customer's name; a message rewrites the stretch it falls in from that span's messages, joining the stretches it bridges (`writeTimeline`), and a link change writes every stretch again (`rewriteTimeline`), so redelivery, history arriving late and relinking agree. Lines a day from before are written again as stretches once at start (`upgradeTimeline`, for a customer with a line that has no `from`). The client's line (`WhatsAppEvent`) adds the stretch's first and last days when they differ. `historyOf` passes its `changes` through; the client draws it as `WhatsAppEvent` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`, the customer's name in the actor slot, opening the WhatsApp tab. `lastExchanges` counts `whatsapp` rows as contact (`exchanges.ts`), and a lead with one turns working (`leadWorked`). Converting a lead moves its customers to the new contact, merging moves them to the lead kept, deleting a lead or contact unlinks them (`CrmService.whatsappRecords`, called inside those transactions). The media files are kept by the file sweep while a message names them (`file-sweep.ts`), and read through the attachments' content route, which asks the conversation (`CrmService.whatsappFileReadable`).

**Who reads what.** A conversation linked to a record is read by whoever may read that record (`mayRead`); one linked to none by whoever has the page grant `crm-whatsapp` (both sales sets carry it; existing sets received it once, marker `crm-whatsapp-v1`). The conversation endpoints also check that grant; changing a linked customer's link or state needs edit access to the record they are linked to, linking needs edit access to the new one. The connection page is the CRM settings' (`manage`), and so are its endpoints.

**No one assigned, no notices.** While the CRM only receives, replies are made on WhatsApp by whoever holds the number, so the CRM assigns no one to a conversation and tells no one of a message (since 2026-10-16; who replies and how is phase 2's, with sending). Numbers have no receptionist (their `defaultHandlerId` column and the conversations' `handlerId` stay from before, unread and unwritten; the number routes refuse the field) and the menu has no WhatsApp count. The list has no one's own view either: All (the default, every conversation the reader may read) and Unlinked (the Mine there briefly was, worked out from the records the reader owned, went on 2026-10-16; an address naming it opens All, and the API refuses `view=mine`). Customers linked to no record wait under Unlinked for anyone with the page; a salesperson reads their customers' messages on their records' WhatsApp tabs and timelines. The record owner learns of a message from the record's timeline and WhatsApp tab and from the follow-up below. `deliverNotifications` still delivers WhatsApp notices queued before (entity `whatsapp`, event `whatsappNew`; the record's event `whatsapp`), their titles in `server/locales/crm-notifications`.

**Follow-ups.** A customer's recent message on a linked conversation sets `followUpDueAt` thirty minutes on (`QUIET_MS`, ten until 2026-10-16); once it passes quietly, the lead and the customer's open deals are followed up through the same `customerReplied` as a mail reply (one switch for both). The lead and deal follow-ups and task preparation read the record's WhatsApp messages (`whatsappOf`, the last 180 days, at most 30, as the customer's words under "# WhatsApp with …", `whatsappSection`).

**Pages.** The menu's Integrations section has WhatsApp after Mailbox (`crm-whatsapp`, `/whatsapp`, WhatsApp's own mark as its icon, `WhatsAppLogo` in `client/components/crm/whatsapp/logo.tsx`, since lucide has no brand icons, no count): `client/pages/crm-whatsapp/` — the list (`?view=all|unlinked`, and with two numbers or more a number filter, `?number=`, from `GET /whatsapp/numbers` through `useWhatsAppNumbers`, the demo's numbers too, each row then naming its number while none is picked, as the conversation's header does; polled every 15 s) beside the open conversation (`/whatsapp/:conversationId`, polled every 10 s, its header naming the linked record after its kind's menu icon, `ENTITY_ICONS` in `client/components/crm/entity-icons.ts`, which the global search's groups share), its link dialog (`…/link`, prefilled with the current link, which comes back when its kind is picked again after another; a contact picked, it says where the messages also go, the contact's company and the deals naming it, `ContactReach` from the contact's detail; a customer links to a person, a lead or a contact, never to a company or a deal, which they reach through the contact) and new-lead form (`…/new-lead`, the CRM's `RecordForm`); the page hosts the record drawer (`?open=`). On a phone one pane shows at a time, and an open conversation has the screen (since 2026-11-03): no page title or number switch over it, a narrower margin, and a header of a few lines, the way back an arrow beside the name, a button (not yet linked, or set aside) on a row of its own, the number it came in on named by its name alone. On every screen the customer's number follows their name, the company number it came in on reads Our number (公司号码), and a linked customer's Change link sits in the header's menu. Every WhatsApp time and day the pages show (bubbles, day separators, the list, the timeline line's span, the connection page) reads in the CRM's time zone, as the timeline's and the tasks' do, not the browser's (`useBusinessZone`, `dayIn` in `client/components/crm/whatsapp/api.ts`). The messages are `WhatsAppMessages` (`client/components/crm/whatsapp/messages.tsx`: day separators, bubbles, media, Load earlier, a New messages button, pinned to the newest while the reader is there), the record tab is `RecordWhatsAppPanel` (`record-tab.tsx`, on the card's white as the Mail tab's list is, one person's conversation at a time, since the team's replies to two people interleaved would not say whom they answer: the latest to write first and, with two or more, line tabs over a rule naming each with their count (laid out by the head, `data-tabs-layout='own'`, outside the line tabs' shared rule in `client/styles.css`), kept at the top of the column while it scrolls (`sticky`; the head and the body each draw their part of the card's border, so the stuck head keeps its top edge and corners), with Open conversation on its right (for whoever has the WhatsApp page), which opens the shown person's conversation, the newest message's, in a drawer over the page (`WhatsAppConversationDrawer` in `conversation-drawer.tsx`: the customer, the number, the messages read only and polled as the page polls them, Open full page leading to the WhatsApp page) (`GET …/whatsappMessages?person=`, the response's `people` with `messageCount` and `lastMessageAt`, latest first, and `person`), the read-only note under a rule at its foot, on leads, contacts, companies and deals while `whatsapp.configured` is in the workspace metadata, which is true while a number is connected or the demo dataset's number is there, polled every 30 s), failed loads are `WhatsAppFailure` (`failure.tsx`). The connection is `client/pages/crm-whatsapp/connection.tsx` at `/whatsapp/connection` (route `crm-whatsapp-connection`, no menu entry), reached from the WhatsApp page's header "Manage WhatsApp connection" (管理 WhatsApp 连接) for whoever manages the CRM's settings, as the mail accounts are from the mailbox's (it was a Settings tab until 2026-10-15): the Meta app (its secret), the numbers (each with its mode and standing, Check, Edit and Remove, Remove refused for one holding conversations; added and changed in a dialog at `numbers/new` and `numbers/:numberId`, `number.tsx`, coexistence the default and marked recommended), the webhook address and verify token (subscribe `messages`, and `smb_message_echoes` and `history` for coexistence numbers), and the processing, showing only what needs doing: one line while all is well (the latest of the numbers' `lastWebhookAt`, or that nothing has come yet), else the deliveries waiting, those failed with their reasons and Retry failed deliveries, and the files waiting or unavailable, each only when there are any. Copy is under `whatsapp`, `whatsappSettings`, `feed.whatsapp` in `client/locales/crm/index.ts`.

**The demo numbers.** The demo dataset writes two numbers of mode `demo` with eight conversations between them ([crm-demo.md](crm-demo.md)). They receive nothing and are never the company's: `numbers()`, `configured` and the number endpoints leave them out (`ownNumber` refuses them, 404); the connection view returns them apart as `demoNumbers`, which the connection page lists after the company's numbers read-only (a dashed border, a 共存 tag, since they behave as coexistence numbers do, beside a 演示 tag, the business name and the conversations on one line, a line saying they are not connected to Meta, no Check, Edit or Remove); the list's filter and the conversations' number labels (`numberLabels()`) include them.
