# Company signals

Read this before changing the company signals: what the AI finds on the web that happened to a company someone sells to, its daily check, the Signals card, or the web search behind it.

A signal is a change outside the CRM that a salesperson would act on, of five kinds (`SIGNAL_TYPES` in `server/providers/crm/signal-reading.ts`): `funding`, `hiring`, `leadership`, `business` (a major launch, an acquisition, a large contract, a listing) and `risk` (layoffs, a lawsuit, trouble). It belongs to its company (`crmSignals`, migration `202610140002_crm_company_signals`), changes none of the company's fields and starts nothing on its own: in the guideline's terms the AI is the company's collaborator, handing what it finds to the owner. Its status is `new` until someone who may edit the company dismisses it (`dismissed`, the one decision, for an event that is wrong or of no concern, which 取消忽略 in the whole list undoes: `PATCH /signals/:id` with `new`, clearing who decided; `seen`, which once only said it was read, is history and reads as new, and the route refuses it); it reads as new on the page for a week after it was found (`isNew` in `company-signals.tsx`), for everyone alike; a dismissed event keeps its `eventKey` (its kind, month and wording, `eventKey()`), so it is never proposed again, and the events already known are named to the search so it does not repeat them. Deleting a company deletes its signals (`CrmService.remove`), as removing the demo data does for the demo's companies.

`server/providers/crm/company-signals.ts` (`CompanySignals`, `companySignalsToken`, registered by `CrmProvider`) does the work. The daily check, the scheduler task `crm.company-signals` at 08:00, an hour before the deals' daily pass (texts in `server/locales/crm-ai/schedules.ts`, switched off on the scheduled tasks page), looks at the companies with an open deal and those someone switched `crmCompanies.signalsWatched` on, each at most once every `CHECK_EVERY_DAYS` (7) after `signalsCheckedAt`, the longest unchecked first, at most 100 a pass (20 until 2026-10-15; 700 companies in the check before their weeks stretch), one at a time, and none started once the pass has run 50 minutes (`SCAN_BUDGET_MS`, so a slow search does not hold it past the deals' pass at 09:00; the result says `stopped`, and those left are the longest unchecked, taken first the next morning); a company whose check failed keeps its old `signalsCheckedAt`, so the next pass tries it first. Someone who may edit a company may check it at once (`POST /api/companies/:companyId/signals/check`), whether or not it is watched; an agent does so with `crm companies check-signals` (`POST …/signals/request`, `CompanySignals.startCheck`), answered at once while the search goes on, what it finds told back to the asking conversation (`deliverSignalCheck` in `server/providers/crm-agents/ai-work-news.ts`). A check asks one question with the web search on (`signalQuestion`: the company's name, website, industry and city, the last `SIGNAL_WINDOW_DAYS` (30), the kinds, routine news left out, the known events, the AI language) and reads the answer (`readSignals`): an event is kept only when it is of a tracked kind, dated within the window and cites pages the search returned as `[ref_n]`, its sources being those pages, never a URL the model wrote (asked for links, it made up plausible ones). An answer in prose (the model often ignores the JSON instruction while citing) is asked again without searching to put the same events into JSON (`signalJsonQuestion`), its marks still naming the first answer's pages. Each kept signal writes a line on the company's timeline (`crmHistory` action `signal`, source `ai`, actor `system`, its `changes` the kind, title, day and first source, passed through whole by `historyOf` like a comment's) and a notice to the company's owner and its open deals' owners (`CrmService.notifySignal`, event `signal`, under the record-change notifications switch, opening the company page's whole list of signals, `/companies/:id/signals`, which opens the Signals card's dialog). Both calls are recorded on the Agents plugin's usage page as the `Company research` agent's (`recordUse`, `sourceOf('signals')`), whose first model is the one searched with, whichever it is (`CrmProvider`'s searcher; no fallback to another model); its method skill is attached to it, not to the assistant, which cannot search.

The web search is `server/providers/crm/web-research.ts`, in three ways by the agent's model (New company with the AI asks it too, `CompanySignals.profile`, [crm-capture-and-merge.md](crm-capture-and-merge.md), and a lead follow-up's look-up of its company, `CompanySignals.lookUp`, [crm-ai-follow-ups.md](crm-ai-follow-ups.md)). A Qwen model of a service configured in `ai.llmServices` with a key (`dashscope`) is asked through DashScope's native API (`multimodal-generation`, China region, `search_options` with `enable_source` and citations, reasoning off; `@temporary(nocobase-official)`: the Agents plugin's Alibaba provider drops `enable_search`, and the OpenAI-compatible mode returns no pages), which cites the pages it read as [ref_n]. A DeepSeek model of a service configured in `ai.llmServices` with a key (`deepseek`, since 2026-10-19) is asked through the gateway (`languageModel`) with a `web_search` tool of the CRM's own, which calls DeepSeek's Search API (`POST https://api.deepseek.com/v1/tools/search`, the same key, $0.75 per 1,000 searches that find something) and gives the model each result numbered as [ref_n] with its title, site, date and a summary cut to 400 characters; the first turn must search and the last cannot (at most 4 searches of 6 results), with the model's reasoning off (`reasoning: 'none'`, since a thinking DeepSeek refuses a forced tool; a model refusing that is asked again with it, the first search left to it), and the answer is kept as written, as DashScope's, its addresses being data such as a website; a search that fails fails the question only when nothing was found. DeepSeek's own `web_search` (its Responses API, `deepseek-v4-pro` only) is not used: the gateway asks Chat Completions, and that search does not say which pages it read. Any other model is asked through the Agents plugin's gateway (`languageModel`) with its provider's own search tool (`@ai-sdk/openai`'s `webSearch`, `@ai-sdk/anthropic`'s `webSearch_20250305`, `@ai-sdk/google`'s `googleSearch`); those return the pages read but number nothing, so the question asks for each event's page URLs (`signalQuestion` with `urls`), `citePages` turns the answer's URLs that are among the pages into [ref_n] and drops any other (except in JSON fields the question names as data, `urlFields`: a new company's website and LinkedIn page), and the unsearched second ask puts the report into the JSON. A model whose provider has no search (an OpenAI-compatible service, or a DeepSeek or Qwen service added only on the Models page, whose key the CRM cannot read) is still asked: its check fails, not to be retried, saying so. Without any model nothing searches: the whole list says so, checking now answers `signalsUnavailable`, and the daily pass does nothing. A search takes 10 to 25 seconds and a few thousand input tokens (DeepSeek's, 7 to 15 seconds and about 12,000).

The routes are `server/routes/signals.ts` (`GET /companies/:companyId/signals` for whoever reads the company; `PUT …/signals/watch`, `POST …/signals/check` and `PATCH /signals/:signalId` for whoever edits it), guarded with the CRM's paths (`signals` added in `server/routes/crm.ts`). Under the company's title, as a lead's or a deal's AI chip is, `SignalsStatus` (the AI's sparkle, the Company research agent's name, "N 条新", opening the whole list, and "立即检查" at its end for whoever may edit the company while the search is configured, what it found shown after it) and `WatchButton` (☆ 关注 / ★ 已关注, for whoever may edit the company) carry the check's controls, which the whole list no longer repeats. The client is the company page's Signals card under its profile, not a tab (`SignalsCard` in `client/components/crm/company-signals.tsx`, placed in `crm-entity-detail.tsx`; a seventh tab made the company's tab bar too long): the latest three signals not dismissed, each title opening the whole list at it as a timeline line does (and a deal's 最近信号 row at its signal), drawn as the column's rows (no box of their own, a rule between them; the kind, the day and a dot for a new one above the title, with 忽略 at the line's end), and a quiet "查看全部 N 条 ›" link opening the whole list in a dialog ("检查信号 ›" while there is none, the dialog then being where one checks, `signals.openToCheck`) (`CompanySignals`: where the check stands with Check now (an AI button: the AI's tint and sparkle), every signal with its summary and sources, dismissed ones last and faded; 20 at a time, the next page loading as the list reaches its end, `AutoLoadMore`). The route pages them (`GET /companies/:id/signals?page&pageSize`, at most 100; those not dismissed first, the newest events first; with `total`, `open` and `fresh` whatever the page, `fresh` those found in the last week and not dismissed), and `until=<signalId>` widens the first page to reach that signal; each reader asks only what it shows (the card three, the chip, Watch and a deal's row one), and a change reads every page size of the company again (`useRefreshSignals`), which `/companies/:id/signals`, a signal notice's address, opens on arrival; a deal's company card has a 最近信号 row (`LatestSignalRow`, `CompanyCard`'s `signals`), the latest one's title and how many there are, opening the same dialog; and the timeline's `SignalEvent` in `crm-detail.tsx` (its title a button opening the whole list at that signal, scrolled to and lit up a while: `focusSignal` / `takeSignalFocus` in `signal-focus.ts` carry its id, since the list's module imports the page; signed by the Company research agent under the AI's sparkle in the primary colour, its type and title without a source, its sources, often several, being in the whole list); strings under `signals` in `client/locales/crm/index.ts`. The demo dataset writes nine real, neutral events a search found on seven of its companies, with their real sources and dates and summaries stating facts alone (GAC's restructuring announcement, NARI's tender win and patent, Shanghai Electric's withdrawn lawsuit, XGIMI's option plan, Guangzhou Restaurant's convertible bond approval, XJTLU's career fair and its TISC, United Family Healthcare at the China Open; `DemoSignal.company` a project's key or another company's index in `companies`), events with weak sources (a stock quote page, a homepage) left out, with its timeline line (`project.signals` in `demo-content/projects-*.ts`), so the demo shows a signal without a model; never an invented event, since the demo's companies are real.

What counts is the method skill `company-signals` (`ai/skills/company-signals/SKILL.md`, in `METHOD_SKILLS`, so it is put into the Agents plugin's skill library once, and an administrator edits it on the Skills page; `readSkill` reads the library's version first). It is sent as the search's **system** message (`searchWeb`'s `system`), never in the question: the search is formed from the user message, and with the method in it the search went after the method's own words (searching "signals" found companies named after signals). With it, the model also left out sister companies with similar names, which it otherwise reported (神州信息 for 神州数码). The question keeps the rules the reading enforces whatever the skill says.

Each check is a row of `crmSignalChecks` (migration `202610150001_crm_signal_checks`): the company, its trigger (`daily`, or `asked` with who asked), `found`, `nothing` or `failed` with the error, how many it found and how many deal follow-ups it started; each signal names its check (`checkId`, migration `202610150002_crm_signal_check_ids`; null for the demo's and those from before). Deleting a company, or the demo, deletes its checks. The checks are the agent `signals` of the Agent work page (`SWITCHED_AGENTS` in `agents.ts`, triggers `daily`; it is not in `AGENTS`, whose runs are `ai` entries): its switches live with the others' (the daily pass does nothing while it or its `daily` is off; checking now is never switched off), and `GET /api/aiAgents/signals` answers `CompanySignals.overview` in the agents' overview shape: its runs the checks on companies the reader may read, newest first, a check's summary the titles it found or its error; its stats, in the agents' slots, the checks, the signals found (accepted), those dismissed, the deal follow-ups they started (pending), the failed checks, and the share dismissed as the acceptance (忽略率: a high one says it finds the wrong things). The page (`crm-agents.tsx`) names those figures by `agents.signals.stats`, filters a check by `found`, `nothing` and `failed`, and opens a check's company.

A check, the morning's or one asked for, starts no follow-up of its own (until 2026-10-15 a signal worth acting on followed the deals up at once, beside the daily pass, which could follow a deal up twice in a morning; `crmSignalChecks.followUps` keeps what those started and is 0 since). The deals' daily pass at 09:00 (`scanDealSignals` in `deal-signals.ts`) takes the news: an open deal (not paused, won or lost) whose company has a signal worth acting on (every kind but `hiring`, `ACTING_TYPES`, not dismissed) found after its last follow-up that came to something is followed up with the trigger `companySignal`, when nothing of its own (gone quiet, close date near, resume due) starts it first; under the pass's daily cap, never over a card waiting or being worked on, and without the week's wait of the other triggers, since it is news since the last follow-up. `companySignal` is one of the daily triggers (`DAILY_SIGNALS` in `agents.ts`), switched with the deal follow-up's 每日巡检, no longer a trigger of its own. A signal found during the day by Check now waits for the next morning's pass. The deal follow-up always reads its company's signals of the last 30 days that are not dismissed as its material (`# Company news` in its prompt, `signalsOf` in `opportunity-evaluation.ts`), whatever started it.

A signal's kind is a tag of its own colour wherever it shows (`SignalTypeTag` in `client/components/crm/signal-type-tag.tsx`, on the card, the whole list and the timeline's line), from the CRM's tag tones: funding `success`, hiring `info`, a change of executives `warning`, a risk `destructive`, other major news `muted`.
