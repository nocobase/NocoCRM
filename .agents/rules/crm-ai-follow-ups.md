# AI follow-ups and the background model

Read this before changing the lead and opportunity follow-ups, the approval review or task preparation runs, their skills, or how background work reaches a model.

The CRM's own AI skill is a Markdown file in `ai/skills/` (`lead-follow-up`, 跟进线索, written for NocoBase's own sales; it replaced the former `lead-evaluation` skill), which the background runs read as their method (`readSkill` in `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`: the Agents plugin's library's current version, else the shipped file, the text without its frontmatter), which is put into the plugin's skill library once (`provisionSkills`), where the assistant reads it with its `crm_skill` tool, and which the build copies into `dist/ai/skills` (`scripts/utils/copy-skills.mjs`, an `afterServerBuild` hook the application declares in `cli/plugins.ts` as its own entry, named `@nococrm/crm-cli`, since only a plugin entry contributes build hooks; the AI Employee plugin's hook did this until the plugin was removed, and a deployment built without it found no skills); the server reads them beside its compiled code (`SKILLS_DIR` in `ai-model.ts`), not from the application's root, which a host such as NocoBase Studio sets elsewhere; `opportunity-follow-up` (跟进商机: the sales stages and the evidence each needs, risks and gaps, advance / keep at it / pause / lose, with the CRM's pause and lost reasons) is the method of the AI's follow-up of a deal (`server/providers/crm/opportunity-evaluation.ts`, `OpportunityEvaluator`), which shares the lead follow-up's machinery (`tasksOf`, `commentsOf`, `taskRules`, `applyStep` and `planTask`, exported from `lead-evaluation.ts`) and its card (`VerdictPanel` with `entity='opportunities'`, the verdict told apart by its recommendation): it reads the deal, its company, its contacts with their roles, tasks, comments, approvals, the lead it came from and the company's other deals, and looks nothing up on the web, so its findings for the company's fields (`enrich`, cleaned by the lead's `cleanEnrich`, written with the accept or through `POST /api/opportunities/:id/evaluations/:entryId/enrich`, `OpportunityEvaluator.enrich`, as an AI proposal the person may make only on a company they may edit) are always empty but on a revision, which carries those of the card it revises that nobody wrote; it lists every commercial term in the material (`terms`: topic, the term, whether it is beyond the standard, the approval the model says covers it, and for one beyond it `why` it is worth asking for) and no approvals of its own, since weighing which approval covered which term kept a reasoning model thinking for minutes: `checkTerms` checks the terms against the deal's approvals rather than taking the model's word and works out the approvals to propose (`approvals` on the card: topic, the term as its name, the model's why as its description): a term is covered only by an approval it names that is approved or pending, otherwise it needs approval and one is proposed for it, a term whose kind's latest approval was rejected only once something has happened since (`resubmit`, `newsSince`), and while a term needs or awaits approval an advance to contract or won becomes keep at it; the card lists the terms with their standing (`DealTerms`); a next step carries on only a planned task (`tasksOf` gives ids to planned ones alone), which the accept creates and submits as the person to the approver chosen on the card, by default the first active sales manager other than them (`submitApprovals`, recorded as `submittedApprovals`), and is followed up again as the deal's owner once the approvals submitted together (the deal's approvals submitted within ten seconds of one another, `submittedTogether` in `feedback.ts`) have all left the queue and one of them was approved or rejected (`approvalsSettled`; `CrmService.approvalDecided`, set by `CrmProvider`, is called on a withdrawal too, since that may be the last), once for the batch, the card naming each approval and its outcome (`ai.decidedApprovals`, trigger `approvalDecided`); the in-app notifications stay as they are; a submitted approval is reviewed for its approver by `ApprovalReviewer` (`server/providers/crm/approval-review.ts`, skill `ai/skills/approval-review`, started by `CrmService.approvalSubmitted`, run as the approver on the follow-ups' jobs with payload entity `approvals`): the asked terms against the standard with their cost, the deal, its other approvals taken together, earlier decided requests of the same topic on every company the approver may read, risks, what is missing, a recommendation (approve, conditional, reject, needMore) and a drafted comment; the review is an `ai` entry on the approval, listed in the approver's inbox suggestions (`pendingDecisions`), decided on its card (`ReviewPanel` in `client/components/crm/approval-review.tsx`, laid out as a follow-up's card, and asked for first by a chip shaped as a lead's first follow-up button, named AI 辅助审批 as the chip that replaces it: in the timeline (`inFeed`) without a heading of its own, the box redoing it, Discuss, Ignore and the accept, which approves or rejects as advised and says so on hover, at its foot under a rule, `POST /api/approvals/:id/reviews/:entryId/decide`), asked again through `POST /api/approvals/:id/review` (the approver's alone, as is reading it: `latestReview` returns none and `historyOf` leaves the approval's `ai` and `aiRevise` entries out for anyone else, administrators and the applicant included, since it reads with the approver's access and leans on a decision not yet made), and retired (`superseded`) when the approval is decided or withdrawn elsewhere, recording what became of it (`ai.approvalOutcome`, `retiredAt`, `retiredById`) so the approver's inbox suggestions keep it among the handled with who withdrew or decided it, and recommends advance (to a stage; won only on a signed contract), continue, pause (a reason and a resume task) or lose (a reason and, when worth it, a revisit task), with a confidence, risks and `updates` the person ticks (`DealUpdates`): the deal's amount, currency, expected close date, business type, source and description that its material states and it leaves empty or contradicts, each checked against what the field takes; and `people`, the customer's people the material shows a part for, which `proposedContacts` matches to the company's contacts as the opportunity capture does (`matchCompany`) and keeps only where it changes the deal: a role to set or change, a contact of the company to add, or a person to create as the company's contact (`contacts` on the verdict, `DealContacts` on the card), the ones the person keeps written with the accept (`writeContacts`, `ai.keptContacts`); accepting writes them through CrmService as an AI proposal. It runs when a conversion opens a deal (the dialog's "AI follows up the opportunity at once", `followUp` on `POST /leads/:id/convert`, and a plan's conversion step, through `CrmService.opportunityOpened`), when someone asks on the right of the deal's tab row, and after an outcome recorded on one of its tasks (`recordOutcome` follows up the deal, else the lead); won and lost deals are not followed up (`opportunityClosed`). The inbox's AI suggestions and their count list deals beside leads (`pendingDecisions` entries carry `entity` and `lead` or `opportunity`), the opportunities board shows the AI state as the leads board does, and the assistant reads both follow-up skills from the library (`crm_skill`); a first follow-up never shows changes since last time; what starts a follow-up besides a person asking is recorded on its entry as `ai.trigger` and said on the card: a task set to completed anywhere (its outcome dialogs follow up themselves; the update route and a plan's update step call `CrmService.taskCompleted` (`afterUpdate` in `server/providers/crm/record-hooks.ts`), which `followUpAfterTask` in `feedback.ts` turns into a follow-up of the task's deal, else its unconverted lead), a lead's company or website changed by a person through the update route or a plan (`leadCompanyChanged`), and the daily lead signals of `server/providers/crm/lead-signals.ts` (a new lead uncontacted for `uncontactedDays`, a working one with no task for `leadStaleDays`, a nurtured one whose review task is due; the days are the CRM settings' reminders, which the dashboard and the business guidance, filled in from them as `crmGuidance(days)` in every Agents conversation's rules, use as well), run as the lead's owner by the scheduler plugin's task `crm.lead-signals` (target `app.crm-lead-signals`, 09:00 in the CRM's time zone at boot, registered in `CrmProvider.boot`): unlike the minute-by-minute passes, a daily pass is something administrators want to see and switch off, and keeps one occurrence a day; a lead with a card waiting or running is skipped, the same signal does not start again within 7 days unless that follow-up failed, and a pass starts at most 20; the open deals get the same daily pass (`server/providers/crm/deal-signals.ts`, task `crm.deal-signals`, target `app.crm-deal-signals`: a deal with no task for `leadStaleDays`, one whose expected close date is within `closeReminderDays` or past, a paused one due to resume, triggers `silent`, `closeDue`, `resumeDue`), and both tasks are registered with their titles and descriptions in the CRM's working language (`server/locales/crm-ai/schedules.ts`, by the AI language setting, else the application's default locale), because the scheduled tasks page shows them as stored. The skills are their `SKILL.md` files alone (each declares no tools, `tools: []`): the CRM's Sales skills page and the company's notes on the skills are gone (the stored `skill-comments` row in `crm_settings` is left unread), and every permission set lost the page's grant and the `manageSkills` capability once at boot (`withoutSkillGrants`, marker `crm-skills-removed-v1`), before the authorization plugin scans the stored grants and refuses an action no longer declared.

A lead created through the create route or a plan's create step (not an import or the demo data, which would each run the model per row, nor a lead created with `followUp=false`, which the lead form and the capture review send when their "AI follows up at once" option, `FollowUpOption`, is unticked) is followed up by the AI at once (`server/providers/crm/lead-evaluation.ts`, `LeadEvaluator`, run as a task of the jobs service — `server/providers/crm/follow-up-jobs.ts`: lead and deal follow-ups share the `crmFollowUps` jobs configuration in `server/config/jobs.ts` (memory, four at a time, its pending tasks written to storage on shutdown, so a restart resumes them; a deployment raises `concurrency` or moves it to Redis in `config.yml`), a task carries only its card's id and does nothing once the card is no longer running, an application stopping mid-run hands the task back (`JobInterruptedError`) rather than failing the card, and a card's `ai.startedAt` tells one being worked on (failed after ten minutes) from one waiting its turn ("排队中", failed after a day, its task lost to a killed process); on the memory queue, the cards still running when the application starts are queued again (`runningCards` in `follow-up-jobs.ts`), since a shutdown cut short (a forced exit, a killed process, every `pnpm dev` restart with a model call under way) writes none of its tasks to storage, and one run of a card at a time (`oncePerCard`) keeps a card whose task was kept from running twice; without the jobs service, as in tests, follow-ups queue in the process one at a time; the code keeps the name evaluation, the interface says 跟进线索, and a person asks again with the button on the right of the lead's tab row, never by itself): the run asks the model gateway (`openSession` in `ai-model.ts`), with the material read as the lead's owner, given the `lead-follow-up` skill as its file states it (`readSkill`), the lead's tasks and comments, its previous recommendation, and the CRM records the lead may concern, gathered beforehand with owners named and no ids, and returns a verdict (follow, nurture, convert or invalid, a grade kept unless new facts change it, a one-line reason, up to three reasons, what to prepare and ask, the edition, the next contact, the opportunity to open when converting, the days until a nurture review (the CRM setting `nurtureReviewDays`, 90 by default, when the customer named no time), and what changed since last time). Accepting a follow plans the contact with the preparation in its description, a nurture plans a review task, and a conversion is accepted only once the lead is converted (`convertFirst`): the card opens the conversion dialog with the proposed opportunity and records the decision after it; the dialog's new company and new contact list every field the server copies from the lead, each changeable by a double-click (`EditablePreview` in `client/components/crm/conversion.tsx`, sent as `company` and `contact`, which `convertLead` lays over the lead's values), and the deal takes a currency and an expected close date beside its name and amount (`opportunity`, of which `convertLead` takes only those two); while it waits the card lays out what the dialog will start from (`ConversionPlan`: the company and contact it would create or link, the opportunity, and the duplicate check from `conversionPreview` in `server/providers/crm/conversion.ts`, which matches contacts by e-mail, phone or name at a matching company and returns the open opportunities of the matching companies, which the dialog also warns of for the chosen company). The verdict is an entry in the lead's activity feed: `crmHistory` gained `source` (empty for a person's edit, `assistant` for an AI proposal a person confirmed — a plan carried out and an accepted verdict set `Actor.via` — and `ai` for the AI's own work), `aiStatus` (running, pending, accepted, dismissed, failed; an entry running for ten minutes reads as failed, since a restart loses the queue) and `ai` (`202609300001_crm_history_ai`; `server/providers/crm/ai-entries.ts` reads them).

A lead created from the AI review posts to `leads?via=capture`, so its creation is recorded with the source `capture` (labelled "AI organised", among the AI entries of the feed's filter) rather than as an AI proposal. The prompt spells out the verdict object and says the skill's own output section does not apply, because a model (GPT 6 Astra) wrote that Markdown layout instead of the structure asked for; an unreadable reply is asked once more in the same session. The follow-up's model searches nothing; when due, the company is looked up on the web first (`server/providers/crm/lead-research.ts`, since 2026-10-19), one question asked with the Company research agent's search (`CompanySignals.lookUp`, so its first model, its three ways of searching and its usage, [crm-signals.md](crm-signals.md)): who the company is, what changed there in the last 90 days (hiring for IT or digitalisation roles, new business, funding, reorganisation, trouble) and whether it uses NocoBase, never a person, by the lead's company, website and email domain (a public mail service's left out; a lead naming no company is not looked up), the answer citing its pages as [ref_n]. It is due (`researchDue`) for a company never looked up (an older card's `researchedAt` counts), on `companyChanged` and `nurtureDue` always, on `uncontacted` and `silent` once the last look-up is over 30 days old, and otherwise once the last look-up, or a person's change to the company's fields (`updated` entries), is over 90 days old; never for a revision. The answer, cut to 6,000 characters, and every page it cites (up to 80; a DeepSeek look-up cited 42), then others it read while there are fewer than 20, are kept on the card as `ai.research` with `researchedAt` (on a failed card too, so the next run reads it rather than searching again); a run that does not search gives the model the latest look-up still under 90 days old, dated and marked as an earlier follow-up's, and a revision keeps the card's it redoes. A look-up due but not made sets `ai.researchMiss`: `unavailable` (no model of that agent searches) or `failed` (the search failed, logged), and the follow-up goes on without it. The card (`WebResearch` in `client/components/crm/lead-evaluation.tsx`) folds a look-up into a line, "{time} 联网查了公司 · N 个来源", opening on the answer as Markdown, its marks linking their pages in a new tab (`Markdown`'s `newTab`), and the pages listed; a miss reads as one line saying why. `enrich` holds the lead's own fields (name, company, job title, phone, email, source, the company's particulars) that its original message, tasks, comments or mail state and it leaves empty or contradicts, each with `basis` naming that material, and the company's particulars this run's look-up states, each with the page as `ref_n` that `citedFindings` turns into that page's URL (a value naming no page the look-up read is dropped, and with no look-up this run nothing from the web is offered), cleaned against the lead's option lists and geography (`cleanEnrich`, with the lead's current value); the card heads them 联网查到 when every one is from the web, 建议更新的字段 otherwise, and they are written only as the person picks on the card (`POST /api/leads/:id/evaluations/:entryId/enrich`, `LeadEvaluator.enrich`, once per evaluation, as an AI proposal). A run that is not a revision still carries the web findings of the last look-up nobody wrote (`unwrittenFindings`, those still differing from the lead, with that card's `ai.researchedAt`), so a person asking again loses none, this run's own finding for a field coming first; a revision carries those of the card it revises. The shipped skill says the same (`ai/skills/lead-follow-up`); an installation keeps the library's copy, which the prompt overrides. `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts` is how every background run reaches a model: the lead and deal follow-ups, the approval review, the task preparation, both captures, the merge proposal (`lead-merge.ts`) and the write-up of a task's outcome (`organiseFeedback` in `feedback.ts`). `crmModel(container, work)` picks the model and the instructions a work follows: those of its work agent (`server/providers/crm-agents/work-agents.ts`, the CRM's own since the Agents plugin 1.0 keeps no online agents: `Lead follow-up` for the lead follow-up, both lead captures and the merge proposal, `Opportunity follow-up` for the deal follow-up and the deal capture, `Approval review`, `Task assistant` (任务助手, named for both of its works, unlike the Agent work tab Task preparation) for the preparation and the outcome write-up, and `Company research`), set up on the Agent work page by whoever manages the settings (Set its model and instructions, `WorkAgentSetup` in `client/components/crm/work-agent-setup.tsx`, `GET` and `PUT /api/aiAgents/:agent/setup`, kept in the `crmSettings` row `ai-work-agents`): the model it asks, while an enabled service offers it (a model none offers is refused when chosen), else the default, DeepSeek's fast one (DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, `FIRST_MODEL`; a model that reasons at length, Qwen Max, spent minutes thinking over each follow-up) else the first chat model, the company research's among the models whose provider can search, since it searches with its model ([crm-signals.md](crm-signals.md)); and its instructions, the shipped ones (`workInstructions`: its task and the rules of `BACKGROUND_INSTRUCTIONS`, in English as the CRM's prompts and skills are) until an administrator writes over them (Restore the default goes back); its method skill is the library's (`readSkill`); its name and description show in the viewer's language, through `nameText` (`crm.agentWork.names.<work>`) and `descriptionText` (`crm.agentWork.<work>`). What an administrator had set on the plugin agents an earlier release made for the work (their first model, and their instructions where they had changed them; marker `agents-work-v1`), or on the Background AI before them (`background.ts`, marker `agents-background-v1`), was taken over once at start (`adoptLegacyWorkAgents`), an agent archived by a person before the upgrade left out; the plugin keeps those agents as disabled rows. The CRM names the agent wherever it does its work, from the workspace metadata's `workAgentNames` (each work's name and `nameText`, translated by the plugin's `useAgentText`, and the model it asks now), and falls back to the AI's own words while that is not loaded: the captures' and the merge's dialogs and a task's outcome box say "由 <agent> 整理" beside their AI 整理 button (提出 for the merge; `WorkAgentLabel` in `client/components/crm/work-agent.tsx`), the follow-up options read "创建后运行 <agent>", "合并后运行 <agent>" and "记录后运行 <agent>" (`FollowUpOption`, `OutcomeBox`'s `followUpBy`: the deal's agent for a task of a deal, else the lead's), and a record's AI panels (`useAgentWords` in `client/components/crm/work-agent-name.ts`, `agentWork.words.*`) name it on the first button ("运行 线索跟进", "运行 任务助手", "运行 审批助手"), the chip, the rerun ("重新运行 …"), the card's title ("… 的建议", "… 的准备"), its progress line and its pin, and as the actor of the AI's lines on the timeline (a signal's the Company research agent), by its current name, the name led by its avatar as the Agents page draws it and set in bold black among the words (`font-medium text-foreground`; in a button, a chip or a tooltip, `AgentWords`' `inButton`, their own colour and no avatar, the AI's sparkle leading them) (`AgentSentence` and `AgentWords` in `work-agent.tsx`, a centred row whose gaps stand for the spaces; an attribute or a pin's title takes `useAgentWords`' text alone); the list and kanban status tags ("AI 跟进中"), the inbox's AI group and the badges on a person's change ("AI 提议", "AI 整理") keep the AI's own words. `aiAvailable` says whether there is a model at all, and `openSession(container, { timeZone, guidance, work })` opens a session on the Agents plugin's model gateway, null without a model: `ask` sends the next turn (the earlier ones as a transcript, the system prompt stating the current time in the CRM's time zone followed by the business guidance, `settingsGuidance(settings)` in `server/providers/crm/guidance.ts`, the use recorded under the work's agent's name as its source (`sourceOf(work)`), which the Agents plugin's usage page lists as written under its model calls outside runs, since the work is never a run of the agent), and `askJson` appends the zod shape as JSON Schema and asks once more on a reply in another shape; `readSkill(name)` is a skill's text without its frontmatter, `jsonIn` the JSON a reply holds, and `modelFailure` turns a gateway `ModelError` into the CRM's error: code `config` or `auth` as `assistantUnavailable` (409), anything else as the operation's own failure (`captureFailed`, `feedbackFailed` …, 502), both answered as `503 UNAVAILABLE`. These runs create no conversations and call no tools. A follow-up's, a review's or a preparation's turn may take `RUN_TURN_MS` (eight minutes, within the ten after which a running card reads as failed). Each turn is asked with the model's reasoning turned off (`generateQuietly`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)`): the gateway's `generate` cannot turn it off, and a reasoning model (Qwen, DeepSeek) then thought for minutes over a deal's follow-up, past that limit or its 16,000 output tokens, giving no answer; so the turn takes the gateway's `languageModel` and calls the AI SDK's `generateText` itself with `reasoning: 'none'` (`ai`, in `dependencies` at the Agents plugin's version), asking a model that refuses the setting (a 400) again as it is, and records the use in the plugin's `agModelUsage` as the gateway would (`recordUse`, purpose `text`, the work's agent's name as source); every run's system prompt also says how to settle what the material leaves open (`DECIDING`: the newest source wins, a close call takes the cautious reading, each point is weighed once). A run the limit cuts off records its error as `timedOut` (`runFailure` in `ai-model.ts`; the gateway reports it as a stopped call), which the failed card explains after its own words, pointing to a faster model first on the work's agent (`AiFailure` in `client/components/crm/ai-failure.tsx`); any other failure keeps the provider's words, which only the logs and the entry show. In tests, `tests/setup/model-fake.ts` (`fakeModel(container, reply)`, its language model `languageModelOf`) stands in for the gateway, and `tests/logic/ai-model.test.ts` covers the module.

The deal follow-up reads its company's recent signals (company-signals: news found on the web with sources, of the last 30 days, not dismissed) as `# Company news` in its material, as news about the company rather than the customer's words, and its daily pass follows it up with the trigger `companySignal` when its company has news worth acting on since its last follow-up (see [crm-signals.md](crm-signals.md)).
