# Tasks

Read this before changing tasks: their fields, the quick dialog, the task list and calendar, reminders, or how tasks, comments and mail count as contact.

The tasks tab draws its tasks as the task list does (`TaskCards` from `client/components/crm/task-list.tsx`, leaving out the record whose tab it is), and the approvals tab lists its records as such links (`ApprovalItem`, one line in the card, the applicant and approver by icon, their labels on hover), so an entry opens in the drawer and is edited there rather than expanding in place.

Tasks, comments and mail are the CRM's three kinds of work on a record; a comment is written only in the timeline, companies and contacts having lost their Notes field, whose text `202610080003_crm_record_notes_to_timeline` turned into a comment by the record's owner. A task (`crmTasks`, entity and permission resource `tasks`, page `crm-tasks`, created by `202610080001_crm_tasks`, which dropped the former activities with their data) is something to do: a title is all it needs; the owner defaults to its creator; a time (`dueAt`: a time of day, or a date alone kept as the last instant of that business day, which `isEndOfDay` in `server/providers/crm/zone-time.ts` tells apart, there being no `allDay` column), participants (colleagues only; the customer's people are not kept on a task), one record to link and a result are optional; it has no type and its status is open, completed or cancelled. A new task opens quick, laid out like Clarify's task dialog in the application's own elements (`RecordForm`'s quick mode; files held over the dialog anywhere show a dashed drop overlay over the whole of it and are attached on release; a form's files are drawn as a comment's, `CommentFiles`, removed at once on a new record and after a confirmation on a saved one): no heading of its own (named for screen readers only), a borderless task title and a description that grows with its text up to half the screen (the dialog with it) and also takes files dropped or pasted into it (uploaded as the task's attachments and listed under it), then a row of chips: the time (`TaskTimeField` at size `sm`), the owner searched among the salespeople (`TaskSearchChip` in `client/components/crm/task-quick.tsx`, which filters the options it is given when no search callback is passed and lists the first eight before anything is typed), and the searching chips of `client/components/crm/task-links.tsx`: the one record the task concerns (the chip showing its kind's menu icon), found by one search across leads, companies and opportunities and grouped by kind (a lead, a company, or a deal, which carries its company so the task shows there too; the server refuses a task linked to an unconverted lead and to a company or deal at once, `leadOrCompany`, when a save sets those links, leaving tasks linked both ways before the rule and a converted lead's tasks, which conversion gives the company and deal, as they are), fetched only once the chip is opened, five of each kind before anything is typed; the participants among the colleagues; a cross beside the record and participant chips that empties them; and a paperclip that picks files; Cancel and Add close it. A new approval opens the same way (`RecordForm`'s quick mode, its chips in `client/components/crm/approval-quick.tsx`: the topic, the deal, searched among those the applicant may edit and fixed when opened from a deal's page, and the approver, among those who may decide, other than the applicant; the searching chips share `useMatches` and `useDebounced` in `record-search.ts`), with Save draft and, once an approver is picked, Submit, which saves and submits at once. Editing a task uses the whole form, the description right under the title; a task's page shows its description under its heading, has Cancel task beside Complete while it is open (confirmed first, its participants told), lists its participants under its owner, a contact has no Tasks tab, and the task's icon is a ticked box (`SquareCheckBig`), the calendar icon staying with dates. No record page shows a System group: its first group of particulars (General on a lead's and a company's page, Details on the others) closes on when the record was created and last changed, read only (`RecordTimes`), and its timeline tells the rest (completed, closed, converted, submitted or decided), one list newest first with every entry's full date and time and no month headings, and the page's particulars sit in the right column as a stack of cards (`InfoGroup`: `bg-card`, rounded, a faint border and `shadow-xs`), one per group on a lead's, a company's and a contact's page (a contact's General, Personal info (gender, phone and email), and Company (所属公司): its company field, which picks it, the person's job title there, whether they are its primary contact and still employed there, then the company's status, website, industry, employees, annual revenue and location in the company page's order and last its introduction, three fading lines read whole in the pop-up beside the column, `CompanyCard`; a deal's page likewise: General, then its Company card; an approval's General, then its deal's card (所属商机, `DealCard`: the approval's deal field, then the deal's stage, business type, amount, close date and owner, and last its description, as the company card ends on its introduction; the related cards' fields, the company's, the deal's and a lead's but its status, are changed in place as on that record's own page by whoever may change it, `RelatedField` with `mayEditField` in `client/components/crm/api.ts`: the object's edit scope, every record or the reader's own, and the fields it allows, never a converted lead or an approval, the server checking each save again) and the deal's company's card; a task's General, then a card of the record it concerns, picked there: its deal (关联商机, with the deal's company's card, titled 关联公司 there), its company (关联公司) or its lead (关联线索, `LeadCard`: its name, status, phone, email, owner and original message, the company being in its name), a converted lead's task, given its company and deal, showing the lead's card after theirs with the link read only, or, linked to none, the three to pick from (关联记录)) and one headed Details on the others, each folding away and open again from its heading, as Clarify's do (remembered per heading in the browser), and each row led by its field's icon (`FieldIcon` in `client/components/crm/field-icons.tsx`) while the headings carry none, its label on one line, cut with an ellipsis where its column ends and then whole in a tooltip (`RowLabel`), its value copied as it reads by a button the row shows on hover (`InfoRow`; not while the row is empty, its placeholder or dash marked `data-placeholder`, or being edited), the column scrolling on its own beside the left one, with no rule between it and the tabs or under the heading; an empty field an editor may fill reads as a muted placeholder ("Add …" or "Choose …", the inline editor's `hint`), and a lead's empty original message as its own placeholder; the tabs (`DetailTabBar`), in the order timeline, tasks, mail, the record's related tabs and attachments last, each object's with its menu icon (`tabIcons`, and the timeline's task and approval entries the same), are underlined tabs on a thin rule across the column, the open one in the foreground over a 2px line of it, the others muted until hovered, the first tab's text lining up with the heading, the original message and the content under the tabs (an inline editor's own 8px pulled out, `-mx-2`), with a count in muted figures on every tab but the timeline (the mail's from the query its tab lists with, `MailCount`); the Tasks tab's list card (which a record's Contacts and Opportunities tabs share: a contact one line, its name then its job title, phone and email muted and cut where the row ends; a deal on one line too, its name and stage, then its amount and owner (led by the owner's icon) muted at the row's end; and a deal's Approvals tab, an approval its topic, name, status and the reader's side, its applicant and approver by icon at the row's end), the Mail tab's list of conversations, on the list pages the table (its header the template's, a band of the hover grey (`--hover`, which the menu's current entry takes too) in firm semibold words, every table's alike through `client/styles.css`, the calendar's weekdays on the same grey, `SortableHead` showing a column's arrow only once it sorts or is hovered, its rows a little tighter), the board's cards (its cards giving under the name one particular a line, each led by its field's icon on the detail pages (the owner, a company's industry, when it last changed), its columns having no border, only the muted grey at 70% (`bg-muted/70`), a card held over one marking it as a focused field is marked, a half-strength line of the ring colour and a faint halo, its cards as the UI Library kanban's (rounded-lg, a faint ring, spacing of 3 units, 2 between cards) lifting under the pointer; the board itself, wider than the page, moved sideways by its bar across, which alone always shows, thicker, `scroll-shown` in `client/styles.css`, or by holding its empty space and dragging, as Trello's, `useDragPan` in `client/components/crm/drag-pan.ts`, a press on a card or anything clickable left to it) and the calendar (its weekdays on the same band as a table's header, mixed opaque since its grid's lines show through the gaps; `client/pages/crm-list.tsx`; the companies list shows type, employees and country beside the status, industry and source, the leads list job title and country beside the name and company, the opportunities list its business type after the stage; a list's columns are its object's `columns` in `server/providers/crm/schema.ts`), and the overview's cards (`client/pages/crm-dashboard.tsx`, its metric cards in `common.tsx` and its trend chart) have the same faint border (`border-border`, which every border in the application now draws: the light themes' `--border` is the faint line itself, `oklch(0.9 0 0 / 60%)`, in `compact.css` and `default.css`, so the menu's, the header's, a table's and a divider's lines match the cards' and no class adds its own opacity (the sidebar's lines under its brand and over its footer included, which were `border-sidebar-border/70`); form fields keep `--input`; the board's and the overview's cards drop the Card primitive's ring) and the same shadow, and a task reads on one line from a 32rem-wide list; every tab but the timeline and the attachments opens with one line as the Mail tab does (`TabHeading` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`): what it lists, muted, on the left (keys `tabScope.*`), and the way to add one, when the reader may, on the right. A task has one time and no end, and `202610080001_crm_tasks` creates no `allDay`, `endAt` or `externalParticipants` column: `TaskTimeField` (`client/components/crm/task-time-field.tsx`; also a task's inline time on its page, opened by a double-click with its calendar showing and saved as it closes, the last pick read from a ref since the pick and the close come in one event) is the application's `DatePicker` (`client/components/date-picker.tsx`, shadcn's Date Picker composition, with an `aside` beside the calendar and a `display` for its button) with an hour and a minute column beside the calendar (`TimeColumn` from `datetime-input.tsx`, the hour opening at 09, one step an hour and a minute, never typed or a native time control), "No time" going back to a date alone. The tasks page opens on the list, the calendar beside it (`view` in `client/pages/crm-list.tsx`). The task list shows its tasks as the other list pages show their records, a table in the template's frame (`overflow-hidden rounded-lg border`, its rows on the cards' white, an application change to the template's rows on the page's grey in `client/styles.css`) under the same header with aligned columns (title with its box to tick, the record it concerns, a deal by its own name with the whole title on hover, time, owner, participants; `TaskTable` in `client/components/crm/task-list.tsx`, a record's Tasks tab keeping the cards of `TaskCards`), the tasks to do grouped by when they are due in the business time zone (overdue, today, tomorrow, later this week, no time, later, `dueGroups`) and the completed and cancelled ones after them as Closed (latest first, `excludeStatus=open`), each group folding away and back from its heading, remembered in the browser (`nococrm:task-groups-folded-v3`; only closed folded at first), a row's empty space opening its record as on every list page (`openOnRowClick` in `client/components/crm/row-open.ts`), within this year without the year (`dueText`): the open tasks, soonest first and untimed last, then the completed and cancelled ones in the Closed group (there are no Not done and Closed tabs any more), the calendar putting its month stepper left of the view switch (its month a button opening a picker of the year's months, a year at a time, This month at its foot, `MonthPicker` in `client/components/crm/month-picker.tsx`), the heading carrying no total, the next page loading as the list's end scrolls into view (`AutoLoadMore` in `client/components/crm/auto-load.tsx`, which every paged list but the tables uses: the boards' columns, a record's tabs, timeline and comments, the dashboard's lists and the record pickers; a calendar day and an attachment list, which expand rather than page, keep their buttons); tasks have no import or export; a box ticks a task off through the same outcome dialog as the task page's Complete (`RecordOutcomeDialog`) or opens a completed one again, a cancelled one shows a mark instead of a box, and both read struck through, as they do faded on the calendar, its second view; a status picked in the filters lists that status alone, without tabs; and whose tasks (the team's, or mine: owned or taken part in, `involvedId`, the one a task list opens on) is a segmented control in the header, its everyone's choice labelled Team on every list, for the list and the calendar alike, as whose records is on the other list pages (`scopes` in `client/pages/crm-list.tsx`: all or mine on leads, opportunities, companies and contacts, and on approvals the team's or mine, those I asked for or decide, `involvedId`, as a task is mine whether I own it or take part in it, the approval list opening on mine as the task list does; a board's card opens its record from anywhere but its links, under the pointer taking the hover grey and a firmer shadow, and never pans the board), for every view; only timed tasks are reminded of (`remindTasks`, setting `taskReminderMinutes`, switch `notifyTaskReminders`). A call or a meeting that took place is a comment; an email is never a task. Mail reaches each record's timeline by itself: `server/providers/crm/mail-timeline.ts` (`MailTimeline`, token `mailTimelineToken`) reads every user's mailboxes through the Mail plugin as that user, from where the last pass stopped (180 days back the first time, the cursor per user in `crmSettings` row `mail-timeline`), and writes one `crmHistory` entry per conversation and record (action `mail`, source `mail`, `sourceKey` the mailbox and conversation, `occurredAt` its latest message, columns from `202610080002_crm_history_sources`), matching a not yet converted lead by its email, a contact by its email (and through it the contact's company and the deals it is on) and a company by its website's domain, never the team's own addresses. The entry holds a summary (subject, message count, who wrote last, the customer's people) that everyone who may read the record sees; the messages stay in the mailbox, whose owner opens the conversation on the record's Mail tab from the entry (`MailEvent` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx`, `RecordMailPanel`'s `focus`). The mail from before a record existed is one entry at the end of its timeline instead of one per conversation, so a long correspondence before a lead was created does not bury the record's own story: `CrmService.historyOf` leaves those entries out of the paged list and adds a `mailBefore` entry on the page where the list ends, holding their count and the latest one's time, which `EarlierMail` in `client/pages/crm-detail.tsx` draws as one line whose count opens the Mail tab, where they are listed. The Mail plugin tells only the browser of a change, so `CrmProvider` runs the pass once a minute and the browser asks for its user's own after sending (`POST /api/mailTimeline/sync`), marked `@temporary(nocobase-official)` until the plugin publishes a server-side event. A completed task, a comment or a mail entry is contact (`lastExchanges` in `server/providers/crm/exchanges.ts`), which the statistics, the dashboard's risks and the daily lead and deal checks read, and which turns a new lead working (`CrmService.leadWorked`); planning a task does not. A customer's reply on a lead or an open deal starts a follow-up as the record's owner (trigger `customerReplied`) unless a card on it is waiting or running. An installation from before keeps its permission sets: their grants on the activities operation, page and table are renamed to the tasks ones once at boot (`taskGrants`, marker `crm-tasks-v1`), since the authorization plugin refuses to start on a grant it no longer knows. An AI next step is a task or an email (`STEP_TYPES`): an email step plans no task. Its draft opens with a greeting addressing its recipient by name and ends with a closing line and the owner's name (the lead's person; on a deal the contact the material marks `addressee`, its primary contact with an address, else its first; the lead prompt names the owner), and while the card waits it is laid out to be changed on the card (`EmailStep` in `client/components/crm/email-step.tsx`, in place of the accept button): from (the reader's mailboxes), to and cc (the lead's address; a deal's addressee, the others on it copied), subject and body, sent with Send email through the Mail plugin's `sendMessage` as the reader, with an idempotency key per card, which accepts the card once the provider takes it (a refused one leaves it waiting); the fields ticked on the card are written first, here or before the composer opens, through the card's write endpoint (`POST …/evaluations/:entryId/enrich`, which on a deal also writes its own updates, `writeUpdates`, recorded in `updatedFields` so the accept leaves them be), so a refused send keeps them and the timeline records them, or opened in the plugin's composer for attachments or formatting (given bare addresses: it takes no names, and one sent with an address is refused by the provider); without a mailbox the draft is only copied, and accept stays, named for what it is (Accept: I will send the email myself, `acceptEmailed`), since an email step plans no contact.
