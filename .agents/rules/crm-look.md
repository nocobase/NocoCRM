# NocoCRM look and form controls

Read this before changing the CRM's colours, theme presets, tags, status colours, record forms or date and time inputs.

Every record form sizes its columns by its own width (a container query, three from 42rem), picks a date field's day from the application's `DatePicker` as a task's time is (a calendar in a popover, sent as `yyyy-MM-dd`), never a native date input, as the record pages' inline editor does too (opened with its calendar showing on a double-click, saved as it closes), and shows a field's focus as the record pages' inline fields do, a lighter border and a faint halo (`LIGHT_FOCUS` in `client/components/crm/focus.ts`) rather than the primitives' heavier ring, as every input, textarea, select and input group in the application does through an unlayered rule in `client/styles.css` (an invalid field keeping its red, the AI's ask boxes their glow), so the same form fits the review's middle column; a new lead's form, in the review and in "Fill in a form" alike, shows the fields a salesperson fills first and keeps the company's particulars and attachments behind a "More" row (`LEAD_MORE_FIELDS` in `client/components/crm/form.tsx`) unless they hold a value or the AI marked them.

The CRM's primary is its purple, `oklch(0.55 0.2 290)` in light and `oklch(0.76 0.16 290)` in dark (`--primary` in both presets, an application change to the template's blue), and there is no separate AI colour: AI entry points and proposals (shortcut buttons, the AI's chips, the assistant's send button) use the primary tokens like every other action, and the AI's cards in a timeline sit on the card's white whether waiting or decided, only their accept in the primary. The `--auth-brand-*` colours (kept in the theme files for the template's brand panel, which the sign-in pages no longer draw), the focus ring and `index.html`'s startup mark and `theme-color` follow the same purple. The template's default preset is `compact` (`client/theme/themes/compact.css`, the dense scale the CRM is designed for, `--spacing` 0.225rem and `--radius` 0.5rem as the template's; `default` is the roomier one); in light mode both presets set the pages on the template's very light grey (`--background` oklch(0.985 0 0), so white cards stand off it) and the menu on the cards' white (`--sidebar` `var(--card)`, its current and hovered entries on the `--hover` grey); a hovered button, row, card or list item takes the menu's own grey through the `--hover` token (`hover:bg-hover`; in `default.css`, so every preset inherits it: `var(--sidebar)` in light, `var(--muted)` in dark), the `button` primitive's ghost and outline variants and the `table` primitive's row included (application changes to the registry primitives), while a selected item stays on `--accent` (oklch(0.955 0 0)) and tags and tiles on `--muted` (oklch(0.968 0 0)); a hover inside the menu keeps `--sidebar-accent`, since the menu's grey would not show on itself, and a record page no longer lays its own white over the page, chosen in the header's Appearance popover; the menu's icons take `--sidebar-icon` (in `default.css`, which every preset inherits: the menu's cool hue a few shades deeper, the current entry's icon in its words' colour, and the other entries' words lighter than the menu's foreground (70% of it, mixed toward its ground), a section's label lighter still (50%); applied by rules in `client/styles.css`, the sidebar primitive left as the CLI writes it); both carry the template's light `--sidebar-primary` mix (8% of the primary over the menu's ground, `--sidebar-primary-foreground` the primary: the current menu entry is a soft purple fill in purple words, no outline, a hovered one the hover grey), so keep the two files in step.

`client/components/ui/badge.tsx` carries application changes on the registry primitive: the `ai`, `muted`, `success`, `info` and `warning` variants, and one rounded-rectangle shape instead of the pill; keep them if the primitive is added again. Every tag is the template's small badge, an application change in `client/styles.css` to its larger default for a status standing on its own, so every CRM tag (a status, an approval's topic through `KindTag`, the AI's state, the reader's own marker) is one size and differs from the others only in colour. A status's colour comes from `statusTones` in `client/components/crm/common.tsx`, from the template's `--status-success` and `--status-warning` and the application's `--status-info` beside `--destructive` (the Tailwind colours `success`, `warning` and `info` in `client/styles.css` map to them; `--status-info` is in `default.css`).

A field with a date and a time is edited through `DateTimeInput` (`client/components/crm/datetime-input.tsx`) — in the record form, the inline editor and an AI card's planned task: text in the same `yyyy-mm-dd HH:mm` on a 24-hour clock that `localDate` displays, typed or picked from a popup with the registry `Calendar` (`client/components/ui/calendar.tsx`, react-day-picker) and hour and minute columns. Never a native `datetime-local` or `time` input, whose format and 12/24-hour clock follow the browser's locale, and not the UI Library's `@nocobase/date-time-picker`, which is built on them and on the locale's `PPP p` format; times are entered on the laptop's clock, as the form's hint says, and the inline editor saves when the popup closes, like the other popup pickers.

The primary stays purple, but the buttons that create a record, a lead's Convert, a task's Complete, an approval's Submit for approval (on its page and in the new approval dialog), Withdraw and Approve, and a signal's Seen are black: the button primitive's `contrast` variant (`bg-foreground text-background`, an application change to the registry primitive), on the list pages' New button, the conversations page's New conversation, and every record tab's main button, at the default height of a page's buttons rather than small: New task, New approval, a related list's New (opportunity, contact, lead), a deal's New contact and Write email (black since 2026-10-15, as the other create buttons). Use `variant='contrast'` for a button of that kind rather than a colour of its own. The Agent team and Settings sections (Agent work, Business guidance, General, Users, Roles, API keys, Scheduled tasks, the approvers, the dialogs of `client/pages/settings/common/shared.tsx`, and the Agents plugin's Agents, Skills, Models and Usage pages) use the theme's primary for their main buttons, and every switch is the primary when on: they were black until 2026-10-15. A dialog's footer buttons keep the default size, the size of a page's buttons (`client/styles.css` no longer makes them taller).
