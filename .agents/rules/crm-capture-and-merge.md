# AI capture and duplicate leads

Read this before changing New lead or New opportunity with the AI, the capture review, or finding and merging duplicate leads.

New lead opens with the AI first while the assistant is enabled (`client/components/crm/lead-capture.tsx`; the form directly otherwise; `CaptureTabs` atop both dialogs switches between them, the list page keeping the pasted text until either is closed): pasted text goes to `POST /api/leads/capture` (`server/providers/crm/lead-capture.ts`), which asks the CRM's model through the Agents plugin's model gateway (`openSession` and `askJson` in `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`), the records it checks read as the signed-in user. The capture follows the `form-capture` skill (`ai/skills/form-capture/SKILL.md`, the same text ITAM's AI 整理 ships, so the two Solutions fill forms alike: change it in both together), given as the session's method (`openSession`'s `method`): the request (`server/providers/crm/form-capture.ts`) gives the model the form's own fields, built from the definitions the form is drawn from (`captureFields`: type, required, the options the settings allow), with the labels the dialog shows (`labels` on the request, from `client/components/crm/capture-labels.ts`; a field without one is named by its key) and each field's rule as its `hint` (`HINTS` in `lead-capture.ts` and `opportunity-capture.ts`), the salespeople an owner may be as `records.users`, the form as it stands when adjusting and `today`, inside `<context>`, and the pasted text inside `<material>`; the model answers each form's `values` and `uncertain` (every value the text does not state outright) and `said`, the reply. What is particular to a form is its task: one lead per enquiry, under its `ref` (`ONE_ENQUIRY`: the people of one company about the same need are one lead, its person the one who decides or asked, the others written into its message with their particulars; different companies or unrelated needs are separate leads); the deal's `people` beside its values. Values are checked field by field (an option among the field's, a country and a province resolved, an owner among the salespeople by id or by a name only one has), and a field an adjustment's answer leaves out keeps the person's value. New company follows the skill too, in two steps: the Company research agent's search answers the question (`profileQuestion`), then its findings and pages, as `web` in the context, are read into the company form by the skill with the same agent's model (`readProfileFound` in `company-signals.ts`, `profileTask` and `PROFILE_HINTS` in `company-profile.ts`), every text value in the language asked for whatever the pages are in, and `sources` the refs the values come from; both checked as the search's own answer is (`profileOf`), which stands when there is no model to read with or its reply cannot be read. It saves nothing: it returns prefilled leads, the fields the model was unsure of in `uncertain` and possible duplicates looked up through `CrmService.list`. One review shows them, however many: a list (with several) of each lead's state and owner beside the lead form itself (`RecordForm`'s `embedded` mode, fields only, values reported back), required fields left empty highlighted, owner and source settable for all (filling only what neither the AI nor the user set on a lead), and, in a panel on the right that stays in view beside the form, the pasted text, editable there and organised again on request (which replaces the leads not yet created), above an instruction box that sends the leads as they stand with the user's words to the same endpoint, which returns them revised under their keys (`ref`), every AI field included and null where cleared, plus a `reply`; the AI may name an owner only for a user who may assign, from the active salespeople. Creating takes the checked leads that are complete and leaves the rest.

The capture writes every value in the pasted text's own language, places included: the field descriptions in `server/providers/crm/lead-capture.ts` ask for the province and the city as the text writes them, never translated or romanised (an English example there, Zhejiang Province, had the model write a Chinese text's city as Hangzhou); the province is then turned into its code (`subdivisionCode`, which knows the Chinese names), the city kept as written. Its dialog's button and tab read AI 整理 (`leadCapture.organise`, `leadCapture.aiTab`).

New company opens with the AI too while the assistant is on (`client/components/crm/company-capture.tsx`, `CompanyCaptureDialog`, the same `CaptureTabs`; the name typed carries into the form): the person names the company and may add what they know (a city, a website), and `POST /api/companies/capture` (`server/providers/crm/company-profile.ts`, the question and the reading, run by `CompanySignals.profile` with the Company research agent's web search, so it needs that agent's model as a check does, `signalsUnavailable` otherwise) looks it up on the web, reads what it found into the form by the form-capture skill (above), and returns the company form's values found (name, website, LinkedIn, industry, employees, annual revenue with its currency, country, the province as its code, city, the address without the country, province or city it repeats (`streetAddress`, as a lead capture's), introduction; each kept only when it is of its field's kind, an option among the field's), the fields it was unsure of (marked to check), the pages it read (cited ones first, shown as Sources beside the form) and the companies the reader may see by that name (`sameCompany`). Asked with an OpenAI, Anthropic or Google model's own search (Qwen's and DeepSeek's answers cite pages as [ref_n] and are kept as written), the answer names the pages it used by URL in `sources`, which `citePages` turns into [ref_n] marks as it does an event's, while the URLs of `website` and `linkedin` stay as written (`PROFILE_URL_FIELDS`, passed as `searchWeb`'s `urlFields`): they are the company's own pages, not citations, even when the search read them; a URL ends at a double quote, so a URL in a JSON string never takes the JSON around it. It writes in the language the name was typed in (a name in Chinese characters in Chinese, any other in the AI's language), saves nothing, and the person creates the company from the form, its source still theirs to pick. Its dialog reads 由 公司情报 整理 (`WorkAgentLabel work='signals'`).

The old "Capture lead" shortcuts, which sent a prompt to the chat, are gone, and so are the lead page's follow-up, research and conversion shortcuts, which the follow-up covers. The pasted text is not kept with the lead, as in NocoProject's intake: only the fields the user confirms are saved, and the run creates no conversation that would keep the text.

New opportunity opens with the AI too while the assistant is on (`client/components/crm/opportunity-capture.tsx`, `OpportunityCaptureDialog`, the same `CaptureTabs` beside the form): `POST /api/opportunities/capture` (`server/providers/crm/opportunity-capture.ts`, `captureOpportunity`, by the same skill) returns one prefilled opportunity, its company matched by `findDuplicates` on the company the text names rather than by the model, the people it names matched to that company's contacts (`matchCompany`, again through `POST /opportunities/matchCompany` when the person picks another company, which the embedded form now allows) and the company's open deals as duplicates; the people not matched stay in the form's contacts field as rows carrying `person` instead of a `contactId` (`contactRows`; the field draws them as `NewContact`, marked new) and are created as the company's contacts with the opportunity unless their row is removed; an adjustment sends the field's rows, which the server names (`peopleOf`, a contact by its record) so the model keeps, changes or drops each person. A contact picker in that field does not offer a contact already in another row (`RecordPicker`'s `exclude`). Creating posts `opportunities?via=capture`, recorded as organised by the AI, and the create route follows a new opportunity up (`dealEvaluator().request`) unless `followUp=false`, which the form's and the dialog's "AI follows up the opportunity at once" send when unticked.

Duplicate leads are found and merged in `server/providers/crm/lead-merge.ts` (`GET /api/leads/:id/duplicates`, `POST /leads/:id/proposeMerge`, `POST /leads/:id/merge` with `:id` the lead kept) and `client/components/crm/lead-merge.tsx` (`LeadDuplicates` atop a lead's particulars, a card with the groups' border and shadow on a faint wash of the warning colour, each duplicate one sentence that wraps where its line ends ("Same email as …", 与 … 邮箱相同), ending in Merge, its owner and date on hover, its merge dialog on the registry `RadioGroup`): open leads only, matched by plain rules (the same email, a phone ending the other's, the same LinkedIn profile however its address is written (`linkedinKey`), the same person at a company named alike), never by the model; the AI only proposes which lead to keep and which values are right, the plain proposal standing in when it is off or fails. A merge asks for edit access to both leads, not the right to delete, because nothing is lost: the other lead's values stay on the kept lead's `merged` history entry (which `historyOf` passes through by the lead's readable fields), its tasks and comments move, its waiting follow-ups are superseded, and it is deleted directly rather than through `remove()`; the kept lead is followed up anew (trigger `merged`) unless unticked. The AI capture review checks for duplicates through the same rules (`findDuplicates`, which also lists, without offering a merge, leads of the same company, contacts by email, phone or LinkedIn profile and companies by name) and names beside each lead and contact why it is listed, (same person) or (same company) (`leadCapture.samePerson`, `sameCompany`, by its first `matched` reason), and offers "Merge into it…" beside a lead of the same person: nothing is created; the same `MergeDialog` takes the reviewed values as `input` (the other side, id `new`, `UNSAVED`), the held lead is always kept, only the fields the new values fill are asked about, and the merge saves the picks into the held lead as organised by the AI (`via: 'capture'`) with a `merged` entry marked `captured`.
