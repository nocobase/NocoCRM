# The AI assistant and the Agents plugin

Read this before changing the assistant people talk to (Sales Expert), its dock, ask boxes, panel and conversations page, the business guidance, the conversation rules, plans, or how agents call the CRM.

The assistant people talk to, named Sales Expert, is the Agents plugin's native chat with the CRM's scope, in a panel of the CRM's own (both described with the Agents plugin below). Its dock can be switched off for the whole company on Settings → General (the setting `assistantDock`, the Business defaults card's switch, on by default; the workspace metadata carries it to every page): the header's ✨ button stays. It is reached from the header's ✨ button (`AssistantHeaderButton` in `client/components/crm/assistant-header-button.tsx`, first of the header's actions, an application change to `client/layouts/components/header-actions.tsx`, since 2026-10-16), on every page, the ones without the dock included: it toggles the panel as the plugin's ⌘J does (`toggleChat`), opening it where the person left it, the last conversation, and closing it again, a new conversation being the panel's +; disabled with the reason while the assistant cannot answer; and from the dock (`AssistantDock` in `client/components/crm/assistant-dock.tsx`, mounted in the application layout's `<main>`), the record pages' "Ask AI" having been removed: a box floating over the bottom of the main column (`z-20`, centred, 22rem wide closed and 32rem open; on a phone, below the 768px of `useIsMobile`, a round button in the corner that opens a new conversation with the page's record pinned and nothing sent, `useDiscuss`), hidden while the panel is open, on the overview (`/`, which opens on an ask box of its own in the page flow, `AssistantAskBox` in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask-box.tsx`, with, under it, at most three questions its attention figures raise (`ASK_FROM` in `client/pages/crm-dashboard.tsx`, the most pressing first, each only while its figure is above nought, its count in its words, `overviewAsk.*`, sent as it reads), asking for the judgement the cards below do not give and nothing the inbox already holds (its suggestions, approvals and notices); the same three for everyone it had went on 2026-10-16, and the sales analysis they once ended with is the reports page's header button; composed like the chat's own composer: the question grows past one line (Enter sends, Shift+Enter breaks the line), files are attached with its paperclip, dropped or pasted, and uploaded at once through the plugin's uploads (`useAskFiles`), and the model the new conversation runs on is picked beside them (`useAgentModels`, every chat model of the enabled services, remembered in the browser); both boxes share the draft and the return of focus in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask.ts`, and both wear the AI border, `.crm-ai-border` in `client/styles.css`: the primary colour alone, mixed far toward the card's white so the line reads as faint as the other borders, with a faint AI glow, a thin halo and a short shadow, both firmer while the box has focus), on `/mail`, `/inbox` and `/whatsapp` with its conversations and connection page (filled by fixed panes it would cover) and on the agents' conversations page (`/agent-chat`, which has a composer of its own), outside the application layout (settings included) and while the agents load, and disabled with the reason while the assistant cannot answer (`useAgentReadiness` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/ask.ts`; `usePanelOpen` in `assistant-ask.ts` is the panel's open state). One line until it is clicked, it then opens, a little wider, into the composer the overview's box uses (`AskComposer` in `client/components/crm/assistant-ask-composer.tsx`, the draft in `useAskDraft`): the question growing past one line, the paperclip and the model; it closes to one line once focus leaves it empty, or on a press outside it (a page that drags on a press, the board, keeps focus where it was); while focused it lays a veil over the whole page under it, drawn as a dialog's backdrop (`data-dock-veil`: the backdrop's tint and blur, over the header's z-40, the box raised to z-40 above it while in use, every overlay at z-50 still above both), which a click beside the box lands on, closing it, so the click never reaches the list or the board behind; staying open while its model menu is open or it holds text or files, and not on a switch to another window such as the file picker. Focused, it lists the page's suggested questions above it (by the record's kind on a record page, else by path, keys `dock.q.*` in the `crm` locale); a typed question (Enter, never during an IME composition) or a suggestion starts a new conversation through `useAskAgent`, with the page's record pinned when there is one, and opens the panel on it, focus coming back to the dock when the panel closes (`useFocusReturn`: only when the reader's last press or focus outside the panel was in the box, so a panel opened from the header's button or come back to from the conversations page leaves the dock one line when it closes). Record pages announce their record (`useAnnounceRecord` in `client/components/crm/current-record.ts`, from `LeadDetail` and `EntityDetail`, not from the record drawer, whose backdrop covers the dock; each page withdraws only its own announcement), and the dock's placeholder names its kind ("Ask anything about this opportunity…", `dock.placeholderFor.<entity>`; elsewhere "Ask anything…"). Every page keeps 6rem empty at the bottom of what scrolls under the dock (`CrmFrame`'s `pb-24`, the record page's right column, the board's columns), and a record page's left column, whose timeline ends under it, enough for the box open as well (`pb-36` on its content, which never shrinks to the column; the WhatsApp tab, whose messages scroll inside its card, `pb-20`, the closed box alone, `has-[[data-slot=record-whatsapp]]`), so its last row scrolls above it. The user's conversations with the assistant are the full page `/agent-chat` (对话), which has no menu entry (its route in `client/routes.ts` declares only a `breadcrumb`): the overview lists the latest four under its ask box (`RecentConversations` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/recent-conversations.tsx`, each opening in the panel, an unread one in bold with the AI's dot) with View all (查看全部) leading there; the list the sidebar once kept under the menu (`SidebarChats`) is gone. The assistant reads the CRM through the CRM's own routes run as commands, with exactly the access of the person who woke it, and every change it asks for is a plan the person decides (both under the Agents plugin below). The deployment's business guidance is shipped in `server/providers/crm/guidance.ts` (`crmGuidance(days, language)`, NocoBase's own sales rules and published editions, which the demo dataset follows) and may be written over by an administrator on the Business guidance page in the Agent team section (`client/pages/crm-guidance.tsx` at `/guidance`, checking the settings' `settings/crm manage`, the setting `businessGuidance` saved on its own, Markdown up to a long text's length, blank for the shipped one; the page shows the shipped one, in the AI language with the reminders' days, until then, and Restore the default clears it). `settingsGuidance(settings)` in the same file, which the settings page imports too, gives the one in force; it is part of the rules every Agents conversation on the CRM gets (`server/providers/crm-agents/rules.ts`) and the system prompt of every background run (`ai-model.ts`). The former AI business instructions setting is gone. The approval standards (what discount, payment terms, contract terms and delivery commitments need an approval) are written only in the `opportunity-follow-up` and `approval-review` skills, identically (`tests/logic/approval-standards.test.ts` checks it); the guidance and the follow-up prompt refer to them without numbers. The CRM's former chat service, its `/api/crm/ai/*` routes, the action centre page and the `crmAiMessages` / `crmAiActions` tables are gone (`202609220001_crm_drop_ai_tables`), and so is the AI Employee plugin the assistant moved to after them, with its Registry UI (`client/extensions/nocobase-ai`), the CRM employee and its read tools and `ASK` write tools with their confirmation cards (`server/ai/`), and `POST /api/screenProposal`. The CRM's own AI switch is gone too: the AI is on whenever there is a model to ask, which the metadata reports as `assistant` (`CrmService.assistantEnabled` asks `aiCheck`, which `CrmProvider` sets to `aiAvailable` in `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`) and which the AI's background work, the captures, the reports page's analysis and the global search's Ask AI follow, and so does everything that asks a model: a follow-up, review or preparation asked for or asked again, and redoing a card in a sentence ("Tell AI what to change"); what the AI already produced shows without one (since 2026-10-19, so an external demo with no model shows the demo's): the inbox's AI suggestions and their count, the follow-up, review and preparation cards and their chips on the record pages, each still decided (accepted, dismissed, written in) as it is, the buttons that would ask a model (a first run, Run again at a chip's end, Adjust beside "Tell AI what to change") kept where they are, greyed, their hover saying no model is set up (`ai-buttons.tsx`), while the dock, the overview's box, the sidebar's conversations and the cards' Discuss follow the agent's own readiness; a stored `aiEnabled` setting is dropped on read and on save.

An agent reads the overview and the reports in one call, `crm dashboard` (`GET /api/dashboard` declared as a command, asking the search's action since it reads only what the reader may read; `--scope team` for the team's), and the conversation rules (`rules.ts`) tell it so, list the commands and the shape of their output (`{ ok, result: { data, meta } }`, a get's `.result.data.record`), point it from the attention counts to their records (`statistics`' `members`, then `list --ids`), keep it from the method skills on questions across the pipeline, and have it run independent commands together (as items of one `crm` tool call for the assistant; in one shell call, never cutting output with `head -c`, which splits Chinese into unreadable bytes, for an agent a runner reports): a run that first explored the help and the JSON took a minute where these take half that, nearly all of it waiting for the model between steps. Counts, totals and trends come from each object's summary route (`GET /api/<entity>/summary`, the agents' command `<entity> summary`; `CrmService.summarize`, which reads the rows through the list's own scope and filters, `filtered`, and groups them in `server/providers/crm/summary.ts` by a field, a participant or a period of the business time zone, with sums per currency). Text a model writes never makes the browser fetch another origin: the CRM's `Markdown`, which also draws the assistant's messages, renders images through `client/components/safe-image.tsx`, which loads only this origin's images and shows any other as a link, and `index.html` sets the page policy `img-src 'self' data: blob:`; this closes the image-URL exfiltration that published prompt-injection attacks on AI CRMs used. Neither the assistant nor the background runs search the web (the conversation rules tell the assistant to say so when a question needs it). What a conversation concludes reaches the CRM only through plans, and the conversation rules (`rules.ts`) say how: something that took place with a customer (a call, a meeting, a visit) is a comment on the record it concerns or the outcome of the open task that planned it, proposed in a plan like any change; mail is never recorded (it reaches the timeline by itself), and an email to a customer is a `mail.send` step, to an address recorded in the CRM (never a guessed one) and signed with the name of the person the agent works for (the plugin's `ownerName`, the first rule); a lead's original message is never changed; an AI card in the page context is read from its record's history before it is discussed, and acted on only through a plan (`suggestion.decide`, `approval.decide`) the person confirms. Since some people work in the conversation alone, the rules (and the preset) make the assistant forward: whatever the person says that calls for a change is proposed as a plan in the same answer rather than described for them to do on a page, what is missing filled with a sensible default stated in the plan's summary (asking first only when nothing can be assumed), the obvious next steps added as steps the person can untick, and the answer in the person's language from its first sentence. The assistant's instructions, rules and the way it works with commands are code (`preset.ts`, `rules.ts`, `skill.ts`), resolved when a conversation is created and kept with it: a change reaches the conversations started after it.

The CRM setting `aiLanguage` (labelled AI working language: `en-US` or `zh-CN`, until an administrator chooses one the application's language, `zh-CN` when `i18n.defaultLocale` (APP_DEFAULT_LOCALE) is a Chinese one and `en-US` otherwise, `CrmService.defaults` since 2026-10-19; a stored `user` from before reads as the default) is what the AI writes in when it works on its own (follow-ups, approval reviews, the capture's replies, merge proposals, the daily checks' task titles), the same for the whole team, and the language of the business guidance the assistant's conversations are given; the assistant answers in the language the person writes in, from its first sentence (the CRM assistant preset's instructions and the conversation rules). The business guidance has both versions in `server/providers/crm/guidance.ts` (`crmGuidance(days, language)`), while the CRM's four skills (`ai/skills/*/SKILL.md`) are written in English alone, whatever the AI language: the model follows English instructions and writes in the language it is told. Their frontmatter keeps their title and description as display keys (`i18n: { namespace: crm }`, with entries in `client/locales/`), as the AI Employee plugin's skill pages read them; the Agents plugin's library holds the whole file with its English description (`provisionSkills`), and the body stays English; `tests/logic/approval-standards.test.ts` checks those locale entries, that each frontmatter parses, and that the two skills' approval standards agree. The conversation rules read the settings `CrmAgentsProvider` holds, refreshed whenever the settings are saved (`CrmService.settingsSaved`), since the plugin calls them where nothing may be read.

The Agents plugin (`@nocobase/app-plugin-agents`, registered in all three roots, configured by `server/config/agents.ts`: its CLI is named `crm` and the application `nococrm`) carries the whole of the CRM's AI (the assistant people talk to is its native chat, the background work asks its model gateway, `server/providers/crm/ai-model.ts`, and it replaced the AI Employee plugin). The CRM's agents run the CRM's own routes as commands: a route declares its command with `runCommand(action, options)` in `server/routes/context.ts` (the plugin's `cliRoute`, with a runner agent's run token and the assistant's credential in its security), named explicitly (`leads list`, `leads convert`, `plan create`…), and `crmGuard` takes keys scoped to a run (`required({ scopedKeys: true })`), refusing an action the run does not hold with `ACTION_NOT_ALLOWED` and every non-reading action but proposing a plan with `PLAN_REQUIRED` (`metadata.reason: 'alwaysConfirm'`). What a run of an agent a runner reports holds is `crmActionGate` in `server/providers/crm-agents/gate.ts` (the assistant's, `assistantActions`, the same with the agents' actions, `AGENT_ACTIONS`): the agent's actions, within what the person who woke it holds (`heldActions`, from the CRM's own grants under the run's key scope), within what may be granted at all (`agentActionOptions` in `actions.ts`: deleting, deciding approvals and exporting never; an approval the person approves is decided through a plan they confirm). `CrmAgentsProvider` (`server/providers/crm-agents/`) registers the actions, the gate, the assistant (`assistant.ts`), the instructions and actions every agent a runner reports gets (`preset.ts` and `skill.ts`: the commands, the plan's JSON and its rules), the conversation sources `crm` and `crm-search`, and creates the plugin's model services from `ai.llmServices` when missing, named by their key and titled as config.yml writes them (`model-services.ts`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)` until the plugin reads them itself). Every change an agent asks for is a plan (`server/providers/crm/plans/engine.ts`, `CrmPlans`, `@temporary(nocobase-official)`, modelled on the projects plugin's plans): `POST /api/plans` rehearses every step as the person inside a transaction rolled back (a step refused names itself, `planStepRefused`, field `steps.N`) and keeps it for them alone (`crmPlans`, `crmPlanRows`, migration `202610100006_crm_plans`), the same plan proposed again word for word in its conversation answering the one already waiting (`waitingTwin`) rather than a second card; and a new plan replacing the person's waiting plans it conflicts with, wherever proposed (`supersede`, `conflictKeys`: the same field of a record, the same suggestion or approval decided, mail to the same address; a created record or a comment conflicts with nothing), each marked `superseded` (已替换) with `failure.supersededBy`, so two cannot both be carried out; `POST /api/plans/:planId/execute` (with the revision and the steps to skip) carries it out in one transaction as the person, `stale` when a record it changes was updated since it was rehearsed, and `void` declines it; `PATCH /api/plans/:planId/steps/:position` (`CrmPlans.revise`, the person's alone, no run token) lays new values over those of a waiting step that creates or updates a record, rehearses the whole plan again (a value refused names the step, `PLAN_STEP_REFUSED`, and changes nothing) and moves its revision on, so the card edits each such value as the record pages do (`StepValue` in `plan-card.tsx`, a value naming a record an earlier step creates staying as it reads); a plan carries `titles`, the records its steps' relation values name (old and new) titled by `resolveTitles` as the reader may read them (`CrmPlans.named`), which every value on the card, editable or not, links by, as the record pages do; a plan expires after a day; its steps (`PLAN_OPS`, each shaped in `server/routes/schemas/plans.ts`) create and update records, convert a lead, comment, record a task's outcome, submit, withdraw and decide an approval (`approval.decide`, the approver's alone, as `approvalAction` checks; it retires the approval's review), decide an AI card (`suggestion.decide`: a lead's or a deal's follow-up or a task's preparation, accepted as its card's own button would with everything ticked, through each worker's `decideIn` inside the plan's transaction; the card's step shows the AI's summary, the record's fields it changes and the next task), and send an email (`mail.send`: from the person's first mailbox that sends, or the one named, found before the transaction opens because the Mail plugin reads outside it; the card shows the whole email, `StepMail`; it goes out through the Mail plugin only after the commit, with the idempotency key `crm-plan:<plan>:<row>`, its delivery, or `mailFailed`, written on its step while the rest of the plan stands); an agent finds the cards waiting on the person with `crm suggestions list` (`GET /api/decisions`); it sets the CRM's own agents to work directly, outside a plan, since that changes no record and what they suggest waits for the person (the action `crm.aiWork/request`, `AI_WORK_ACTION`, held by whoever reads records, in the preset and given once to an assistant made before it, marker `crm-ai-work-action-v1`, and let through `crmGuard` without `PLAN_REQUIRED`): `crm leads request-follow-up`, `crm opportunities request-follow-up`, `crm approvals request-review`, `crm tasks request-preparation` (the cards' own routes, `--revise`/`--instruction` redoing a waiting card) and `crm companies check-signals`; the route notes the entry with the run's conversation (`watchRunWork` in `server/routes/context.ts`, table `crmAiWatches`, migration `202610160001_crm_ai_watches`, `server/providers/crm/ai-watches.ts`), and each worker's `finish` calls `CrmService.aiFinished`, set by `CrmAgentsProvider` to `deliverAiWork` (`ai-work-news.ts`), which tells that conversation once: a notice (`aiWork`, `aiWork.news.*` with the work agent's name) under which the conversation shows the suggestion's own card (`SuggestionCard` in `client/components/crm/agent-chat/suggestion-card.tsx`: the record's latest card from its `…/latest` route, `VerdictPanel`, `ReviewPanel` or `PrepPanel` as the timeline and the inbox show it, decided there; one redone from there stays shown, one replaced otherwise says so, `aiWork.replaced`), and the assistant told the result inline (its news, `wakeAssistant`; a runner agent an input that wakes it), so it adds a few lines without restating the card. `crm suggestions show <entryId>` (`POST /api/decisions/:entryId/show`, `showSuggestion`) puts an existing card, the record's latest and one the reader may see, into the run's conversation the same way (outcome `shown`), without waking the agent. The division (since 2026-10-16): the assistant first decides who answers. When the person wants a work agent's judgment on one record (a lead's or deal's next step, an approval, a task's preparation, a company's news), the agent's card is the answer and the assistant's part a few lines of explanation; facts, summaries, questions across records and changes the person names themselves it answers itself, with its plan when a change is called for. Handing over, it only decides whether the latest card still holds (it waits and nothing happened on the record since its `createdAt`: show it; else set the agent to work), redoes a card with the person's words when they disagree (`--revise`/`--instruction`) rather than planning its own, never consults the work agents or works out a follow-up of its own, proposes no plan beside a card (one side gives the card in an answer, never both: anything else worth doing is named in words and proposed only when the person asks), and uses `suggestion.decide` only to decide several at once. This rather than the plugin's consultation, which reaches only the work agents' owner (they are `ownerOnly`) and gives them no commands. `crm <area> mail <id>` (`server/routes/record-mail.ts`, leads, contacts, companies and opportunities) reads a record's mail from the reader's own mailboxes as its Mail tab finds it (`mailOf`), so a reply answers what the customer last wrote; `crm tasks preparation` and `crm approvals review` read the latest cards; before a plan creates a record the assistant checks it as the forms with the AI do: `crm duplicates check` (`GET /api/duplicateCheck`, `checkDuplicates` in `server/providers/crm/duplicate-check.ts`, the capture's `findDuplicates` and a company's open deals), and a company is first looked up on the web with `crm companies look-up` (`POST /api/companies/lookUp`, `CompanySignals.startProfile`, answered at once because a command stops after 10 seconds, what it finds told back to the conversation by `deliverCompanyProfile`), the plan then proposed with its values; every history entry it writes carries `origin` (the plan, the agent, the run) on `crmHistory`; and the outcome goes back into the agent's conversation as news (`plan-news.ts`), the agent told what became of it. The people decide plans on a card, a plan being called suggested changes (建议的改动) wherever the interface names it, since people do not know the word (`PlanCard` in `client/components/crm/plan-card.tsx`, data in `plans.ts`, drawn as the AI's cards in a timeline are: on the card's white, waiting or decided, each step a white card of its own with its fields in a label column, the decision under the steps with "Carry out" in the primary colour): each step's record and changes, an update's old value struck through beside the new one, a checkbox to leave a step out, "Carry out N changes" and "Ignore" (忽略), each with its icon as the AI cards' buttons, a plan being carried out, decided, stale, failed, expired or gone told on its line, focus moving to that line once decided. The card appears in its conversation alone (since 2026-10-16 a record page no longer lists the plans waiting on it, which crowded its particulars; `GET /api/plans?target=` still finds them); it is not in the inbox (no category, no count in its badge), the agent's message that comes with it making its conversation unread, which the overview's latest conversations mark. The conversations with the agents have a full page, `/agent-chat` (`client/pages/crm-agent-chat/`, laid out as the inbox is: the heading All conversations (全部对话, which the breadcrumb shows too) over a rule, Collapse (收起) beside New conversation at its right, returning to the page the reader came from past the conversations opened since (the router's history `idx` when the page opened; opened first, the overview) with the conversation open, if any, in the side panel, the panel's Open full page undone (it replaced a Back on a row of its own) with no line under it, the list in a 24rem column on the card's white (the inbox's list as wide and as white, `inbox-page.tsx`), the conversation beside it, whose scroller reaches into the side padding around it so its scrollbar stands clear of the messages; `/new` to start one with a picked agent, archived ones under `?archived=1`, a plan step's record opening in the record drawer), the assistant panel's full-page view, built like the panel on the plugin's headless chat (`@nocobase/app-plugin-agents/client/chat`) because the UI Library's `agent-chat` block needs a package not on npm; the route's page grant is `crm-agent-chat` (the sales sets received it once, marker `crm-agent-chat-v1`), it has no menu entry, the overview's View all leading to it, and the assistant dock hides there. The plugin's own pages are routed in the menu's Agent team section, after the CRM's four agents (`crm-agent-group` in `client/routes.ts`: Skills, Models, Usage, each shown only to whoever its own grant lets open it; the Agents page, which lists the coding agents runners report since the plugin's 1.0, is routed without a menu entry so the page grants stored for it stay valid). They are drawn in the CRM's look without changing the plugin, whose pages carry their own copies of the registry primitives: `crmStyled` in `client/routes.ts` wraps each one's loaded component in `withCrmStyle` (`client/components/crm/plugin-page.tsx`, a `display: contents` element of class `crm-plugin-page`), and the `.crm-plugin-page` rules in `client/styles.css` give its bordered boxes and cards the CRM's white, faint border and short shadow, its tables the list pages' header band and cell padding, its line tabs a record page's rule, and the usage chart the primary colour; a plugin update that renames those `data-slot`s or classes loses only the look. An answer of the assistant may take 20 steps (the plugin's native chat, not configurable), which is why its `crm` tool takes several commands at once: a question across the pipeline, the reports page's analysis among them, reads statistics, lists and several deals' histories before answering. The plugin keeps its model keys and runners' secrets under the application's master keys, so `SECRETS_KEYS` must be set (`server/config/secrets.ts`); setting it the first time signs everyone out once.

The assistant people talk to, named Sales Expert, is the Agents plugin's native chat (its built-in `online` agent, `OnlineAgentService` since the plugin's 1.0, template 1.0.0-beta.74, which keeps no online agents of its own: the online agent the CRM had made, its work agents and the Background AI are disabled rows of the plugin's `agAgents` since its upgrade) made the CRM's by the scope `crm` every CRM conversation is created with (`configureAssistant` in `server/providers/crm-agents/assistant.ts`, registered by `CrmAgentsProvider.boot`; `config.scope` on creation, `useCrmSubmit` in `ask.ts`). The scope, resolved once when a conversation is created and kept with it, gives it the CRM's system prompt (`assistantInstructions('tool')` in `preset.ts`, the conversation rules of `rules.ts` for the person it works for, `crmRuleLines(settings, name, 'tool')`, how to work with commands and plans, `crmPlansSkill('tool')` in `skill.ts`, the method skills it may read, and the business guidance's sections), every chat model of the enabled services, DeepSeek's first (`chatModels(agents, 'deepseek')` in `work-agents.ts`, as `service/model`: the first a new conversation's, unless the person picked another), and two tools: `crm`, which runs the CRM's command lines (`crm leads list --q acme`, several independent ones in one call, a body such as a plan as its `body`, `crm --help` and `crm docs <command>` from the command manifest, answers JSON cut to 30,000 characters), each one request through the application itself (`app.router.fetch`) carrying a credential the CRM issues for that request alone (`x-crm-assistant-token`, the security scheme `crmAssistantToken`, resolved through the authentication plugin's `addCredentialResolver`, declared in the API document and to the CLI as a run's identity), so the routes check the person as they would any request, and the CRM's guard keeps it to the agents' actions they hold (`assistantActions`: `AGENT_ACTIONS` in `actions.ts` within `heldActions`) and answers every write but a plan and setting the CRM's agents to work with `PLAN_REQUIRED` (`server/routes/context.ts`, where a plan it creates records Sales Expert, `ASSISTANT_ID`, and its conversation, `plans.ts`); and `crm_skill`, which reads a method skill (`lead-follow-up`, `opportunity-follow-up`, `approval-review`, `task-preparation`) from the library as the background runs follow it (`readSkill`), the plugin's native chat running no skills of its own. The `crm` tool shows the conversation its command lines alone (its public projection), which the live line words. What became of a plan, an agent's card or a company look-up is a notice in the conversation (`plan-news.ts`, `ai-work-news.ts`), which the plugin no longer answers; the CRM tells the assistant itself (`wakeAssistant`, `assistantWake`): a message marked as its news (`<crm-news>…</crm-news>`, the rules saying it is not the person's), sent as the conversation's owner once it is idle (tried every 15 seconds for three minutes while it is busy, never again when refused), whose answer streams to whoever has the conversation open; the conversation never shows that message (`isNews` in `agent-chat/news.ts`). Acting for the person outside a request of theirs (that news, the demo's conversations) the CRM names itself as the credential the plugin requires (`assistantActor`).
